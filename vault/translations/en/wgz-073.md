Two groups take part, or a single group is divided into two parties. Two goals are marked with lines, behind which the two parties stand facing each other. A line is drawn to mark the center of the field separating the groups. A large ball is placed on this line. Each child receives a small ball. At an agreed signal, the game begins. All aim with their small balls. The objective is to move the large ball into the opponent's goal. Before the game starts, two ball collectors should be appointed—one stands on the field closer to Party A and, during the game, returns to Party B any small balls that stop there. The other, conversely, stands on the field closer to Party B and returns balls to Party A. These two girls must be impartial. They run in such a way as not to interfere with the aiming.

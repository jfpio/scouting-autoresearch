An escaping group may split into smaller units, rejoin, or use evasive maneuvers such as: walking backward, crossing streams, or marching along their beds (not over a large area, but in a way that allows trackers to find the trail leading out of the stream with relative ease).

The fugitives may perform various actions: lighting a fire, burying something, or leaving behind notes.

Notes may be: incomplete, with torn or scorched sections, copied onto blotting paper, torn and scattered, encrypted, written in Morse code, or with invisible ink (easily done using milk or lemon juice).

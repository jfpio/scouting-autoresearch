A. with blindfolded eyes ("blind" sentinel), B. with eyes open (in dense undergrowth terrain).

Approaching is performed: by stepping or crawling, individually or en masse, by day or by night.

It is also possible to approach a group of sentinels or slip between two sentinels standing at a distance of 20 — 30 meters.

In the case of "blind" sentinels (with blindfolded eyes), their accurate indication of the direction in which the approaching person is located eliminates the latter from the game as captured. In the case of a normal sentinel, calling out the name and indicating the spot where the one sneaking up is located means the capture of the person sneaking up.

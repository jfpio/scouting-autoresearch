A group game. One player stands on sentry duty, while the others are at the opposite end of the playing field or hall. As soon as the game begins, the sentry stands with their back turned to those approaching. All try to approach as close as possible without being noticed. The moment the sentry turns around, the group must stand still. Whoever moves must return to the starting point and approach from the goal line again. The Cub who touches the sentry with their hand before being noticed by them wins.

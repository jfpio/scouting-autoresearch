(Gilcraft, 88/38). The patrol forms a circle. The first person utters a two-syllable word and addresses another, who must then say a two-syllable word whose first syllable matches the last syllable of the previous word. For example: First: Mar-ia, Second: Ia-nek, Third: Nek-tar, Fourth: Tar-cza, and so on. Whoever cannot think of a word loses a point, but only if their predecessor can form the required word from the syllable they provided.

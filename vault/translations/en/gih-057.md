The patrol leader strikes successively a cupboard, wall, windowpane, table, and floor—the patrol listens and watches. Then the patrol turns away—and the patrol leader strikes the same objects again—but in a different order; the boys take turns stating (without looking) which objects the patrol leader strikes.

*Variations:* instead of strikes—tones of varying pitch on a piano, trumpet, etc., or various sounds: clinking scissors, bouncing a ping-pong ball, and the like.

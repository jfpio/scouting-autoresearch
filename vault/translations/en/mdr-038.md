The one chosen as the stag closes their eyes while the others hide. Then, they perform actions: wiping their nose, writing something in a notebook, looking at a watch, and the like. They should make **10** movements. The game then ends, and everyone says what they saw. Anyone spotted by the stag is out of the game.

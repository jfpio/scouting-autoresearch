Each Cub receives two armbands made of coloured yarn.

At a given signal, two packs approach each other through the forest undergrowth. The aim is to encounter a Cub from the opposing party and snatch their yarn. A Cub who loses one armband is wounded; if they lose both, they are killed.

The pack with the most “living” and unharmed Cubs at the end of the game wins.

The Scoutmaster sends the patrol out of the room. Then he takes a thimble, ring, coin, piece of paper, or any small object and places it so that it is perfectly visible, yet in a spot where it will be easily overlooked. He calls the patrol back and instructs them to search. When one of the Scouts spots the place, he must not let it show; instead, he moves to another side and sits quietly. After some time, the Scoutmaster asks him to point out the sought-after object.

One party, sent ahead, conceals itself at a location designated by the leader or chosen by themselves (limit the route by designating a point before which the ambush should be set).

The second party, ensuring its security, marches along the road. (It sends a security patrol as the advance guard and a group to the rear as the rear guard).

The group on the march wins if it discovers the ambush through its scouts. Otherwise, victory goes to the ambushing group. The ambush attack should be directed at the main forces or the rear guard.

An area of field, forest, or part of a city measuring 1—3 km.² is chosen as the "web." Its boundaries are described, and the game's end time is set. One patrol (or half-patrol) becomes the "spider," departs, and selects a hiding place. The other patrol (or half-patrol) departs a quarter of an hour later as the "fly," tasked with detecting the spider; each patrol is accompanied by a judge. If, within the allotted time (e.g., two hours), the fly does not detect the spider, the latter wins. Spiders record the names of every fly they spot; similarly, flies record the spiders, noting their hiding places at once. Patrols should wear distinct colors or otherwise differentiate themselves (e.g., one without jerseys or without caps, etc.).

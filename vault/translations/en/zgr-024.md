A peasant wedding game from the Kutno district (Mazovia), suitable for upper primary school classes and girls' secondary schools. Couples dance in a circle, one behind the other, while in the center stands an (odd-numbered) "shepherd," holding a stick with which they occasionally strike the floor 3 times. At this signal, the dancers release hands and seek new partners; the shepherd does the same. Whoever remains without a partner becomes the shepherd in the next round.

One patrol builds a fortress out of snow, according to their own design. They equip it with loopholes to have a view of the entire surroundings. Once everything is ready, the enemy patrol launches an attack on it, using snowballs. Every scout hit by a snowball is considered 'killed.' The number of attackers should be at least twice as large as the defenders of the fortress.

The participants are divided into two equal even parties. In each party, pairs of friends are formed. The friends are then separated, creating two groups within each party. Next, the instructor takes one group from each party and leads them along winding paths to a certain destination, where they hide as many colored cards as there are boys with them at that moment. After hiding the cards, the witnesses return as quickly as possible to the starting point, where they inform their friends about the location of the hidden cards and the shortest way to reach them. The informed friends then set off along the indicated path to the hiding place of the cards. The party that retrieves the greater number of hidden cards in the shorter time wins. Each informed friend brings back one card. The same game can be conducted between friendly patrols. In case of a larger number of youths, several hiding points for the cards should be created.

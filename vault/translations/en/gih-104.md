Each participant receives a small piece of paper. At the top, they write one line of verse (1 line)—as if the beginning of some work. The paper is passed to a neighbor, and each person receives a paper from another neighbor. On the received papers, everyone adds a second line—rhyming with the previous one—folds the paper so that both lines are hidden, then writes the next line, no longer rhyming, as if continuing the work—and passes the paper on. In this way, a "poem" written by everyone emerges on each paper, with each 2 lines by a different author. When read aloud—much laughter ensues!

Two teams in ranks. Each boy holds his cap in hand. The patrol leader calls out, for example, "b". Each team strives to be the first to arrange itself so that the boy with the cap on his head (representing a dash) and three with bare heads (dots) form the letter "b" according to the Morse alphabet. The first team to do so earns a point, and the game continues until 10—15 points are reached.

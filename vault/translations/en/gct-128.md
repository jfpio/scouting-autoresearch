There are two enemy groups separated by concealed terrain. At a certain moment, they receive news that two aircraft have crashed in their forward area. The task of each group will be to reach the crash site, provide first aid to the airmen (bandaging the head, broken arms, and legs), and transport them to the starting positions. Caution must be maintained, as the enemy may be present in the forward area. The group that finds the airmen first wins. The injured airmen may be specially designated boys. It is permissible to recapture the airman. The recapture can be carried out by a numerically stronger unit (the group may split into smaller units for faster discovery). The airman’s location can be determined using a directional angle.

The troop divides into two equal parties: "fairies" and "witches".

The fairies must carry orders hidden in their clothing to the fairy queen’s palace.

At a certain moment, the witches emerge from ambush and attempt to capture the fairies and find the orders.

A witch may search while counting to 100; if she does not find the orders in that time, the fairy runs away counting to 10, after which the witch has the right to chase her again.

The side that has more orders wins.

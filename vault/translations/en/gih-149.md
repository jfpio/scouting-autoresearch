Two teams sit facing each other. Someone from the first team tosses a handkerchief at one of the scouts from the second team and signals a letter, then asks: "What is the ship loaded with?" The boy who receives the handkerchief must immediately answer with a word beginning with the signaled letter; otherwise, his team loses a point. The team that first earns 8—10—15 points, or drives the opposing team to the appropriate number of minuses (depending on the scoring system), wins.

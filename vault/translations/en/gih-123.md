On a sheet of paper divided into 12 squares, we draw—similarly to the game described above under the title "Examples"—scenes from the lives of scouts, which the boys must identify by the points of the Scout Law when copying the square, writing them into the corresponding squares on the card.

Another variation: the squares contain the texts of the points of the Law—in the appropriate squares, when copying, the numbers of the points of the Law must be written.

I do not think it necessary to add that the inculcation of the scouting ideology in boys cannot end with the memorization of the Scout Law; however, since we teach the boys the Law by heart, it is better to do so in an engaging way rather than a dull, school-like manner.

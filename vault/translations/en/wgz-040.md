A group game for Brownies. The "Roe Deer" stands in the center, with the other girls positioned at a certain distance around her. A Brownie designated by the patrol leader approaches the Roe Deer as quietly as possible. If the Roe Deer hears a rustle and correctly points in the direction with her hand, the approaching girl retreats to her previous spot, and another girl approaches at the patrol leader’s signal. The winner is the one who approaches unheard and touches the Roe Deer’s shoulder.

A dispatch race among relay stations set up over a space of several kilometers.

Approaching the camp according to dispatches sent from a hill or the top of a tree, either by whistle or flashlight. The principle of capturing opponents must be numerical superiority in a given location, so that simultaneous command of all is genuinely necessary. In individual approaches, general leadership is not required, as the ingenuity of individuals suffices.

Each group chooses a hiding place at an equal distance from the pile of nuts (cones). Each Cub ties a sash behind their belt as a tail.

At a given signal, the groups run to fetch nuts (each Cub may carry only one nut) and return to their hiding places, trying to snatch the tail from a squirrel in another six.

A squirrel without a tail is wounded, unless the group redeems it for 4 nuts from the six to which the Cub who captured the tail belongs.

The six with the most nuts wins.

A captured tail counts as four nuts.

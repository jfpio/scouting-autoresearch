A game described by Ł. Gołębiowski in the year 1831, suitable for secondary school girls. An even number of girls form a circle, holding hands, and spin with a dance step. In the center stands one (odd) girl, whose task is to devise a dance figure. When everyone is occupied with performing the figure, the girl in the center chooses a partner for herself, and others follow suit; whoever is left without a pair goes to the center (cf. 1, "The Little Bird").

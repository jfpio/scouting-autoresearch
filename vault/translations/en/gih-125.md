On figures of scouts cut out from paper by the boys, the patrol leader draws colored pencil function cords. Each one in turn arranges these figures according to hierarchy: "who is under whom," meaning "according to the importance of functions." It will be much more fun when the patrol leader colors a certain number of "function cords" at home—then the boys will draw lots among themselves to see who receives which cord—and they will line up according to seniority. This can be combined with a "moral": he who works and is energetic "advances" and becomes the Chief Scout by the end of the gathering.

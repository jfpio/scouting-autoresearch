Enemy patrols operate within an area of 5—10 kilometers by ². They march along roads or terrain lines designated for them based on map 1: 100.000. The assigned march routes may align or oppose each other in direction along certain road segments and frequently intersect. This interconnection of routes can occur at various points and across different lines among all patrols (if there are few) or a subset of them (if there are more than 5). We will complicate this exercise by expanding the operational area, primarily by assigning patrols special tasks within military reconnaissance or regional-natural-economic reconnaissance.

Each patrol must strive to complete its assigned tasks in such a way as to avoid being seen by others while simultaneously observing and monitoring the activities of enemy patrols.

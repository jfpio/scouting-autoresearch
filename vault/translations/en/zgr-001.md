A game widely popular in Poland; suitable for children's gardens and elementary schools. An even number of children form a circle, holding hands; one (without a pair) stands in the center of the circle. After these words, the one in the center chooses a pair for themselves, which all participants immediately imitate. Whoever is left without a pair goes to the center, and the game begins anew.

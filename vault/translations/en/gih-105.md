The patrol sits in 2 rows facing each other. Each person in the 1 row whispers a sentence to their neighbor, beginning with the words: "What would happen if...". In this way, everyone in the row receives a question from their neighbor (each one different), starting with the above words. In the second row, in the same manner, each person receives a whispered sentence from their neighbor as an answer: "Then this and that would happen" (e.g., "Then it would be the end of the world"). After this preparation, the patrol leader points to the first pair—and from it, one person sitting in the first row speaks their question aloud (e.g., "What would happen if the light went out now")—the second person, sitting in the second row, speaks the sentence received from their neighbor (e.g., as above: "Then it would be the end of the world"). From the combination of these questions and answers, funny combinations arise.

A rural girls' game from the Sierpc district (Mazovia), suitable for secondary school girls. The girls divide into pairs (designating one as the "dancer" and the other as the "dancing partner"). These pairs form two lines facing each other, holding hands. Singing the first verse (and the following refrain: one, two, three), both lines glide forward with a mazurka step until they are half a step apart. During the second verse, they retreat to their original positions. On the third verse, they advance toward each other again, and on the fourth, they perform a hołubiec dance with the dancing partner from the opposite pair. On the fifth verse (i.e., the repetition of the fourth), the dancers return to their original positions with their partners exchanged. The game then repeats from the beginning until each dancer returns with their original dancing partner.

A game for rural children from the Kraków and Stanisławów regions (Lesser Poland), suitable for elementary girls' schools and the lowest grades of secondary schools. The girls form two rows facing each other, moving forward and backward. After naming the color of the dress of one of the participants in the second row, the first row joins the named girl to their side. This is repeated until the second row disappears.

2 teams—these are 2 rows, with a distance of 6 paces between them, and the same distance between the boys in each row. The patrol leader whispers a word to the first boy in the first row. He turns around, stands at attention, and whispers (from a distance of 6 paces) the word to the next boy in his row, who, upon hearing it, passes it further. However, the boys in the second row eavesdrop—and when one of them hears it (without moving from his place), he passes the word further in his own row. The row that first transmits the word to the end wins a point. The game is played to 10 points. The patrol leader alternates giving words to both rows.

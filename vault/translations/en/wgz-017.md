Groups stand in rows, facing away from the troop leader. At the troop leader’s signal, the first in each group turns around. The troop leader performs several movements, for example: kneels down, listens intently, places a hand to the forehead, waves above the head. Then, the first ones turn back and repeat the movements they saw, the second ones repeat them to the third, and so on. Silence must be maintained during the game. The last ones step forward in front of the group line and repeat the movements. The group that repeats the movements most accurately wins.

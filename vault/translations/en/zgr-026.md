A game from the rural girls of certain regions of Lesser Poland, suitable for secondary girls' schools. At the start of the game, two circles are formed: one large circle with the "abbess" in the center and her daughters on the perimeter, moving slowly (by walking), and a smaller one with 2—3 "bridesmaids," spinning with a dance step. Here, the bridesmaids detach one of the abbess's daughters and join her to their little circle. Then the process repeats from the beginning and continues until the abbess is left alone.

The patrol divides into several teams of 2 boys each. Beforehand, as many scouts as there are teams step aside and fall down as wounded. At the signal, the teams run to their patients, construct stretchers (from prepared sticks and coats), and race around the room before halting in front of the patrol leader. The team that is fastest and does the best work receives the most points.

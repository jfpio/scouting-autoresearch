The patrol forms a circle, and each member in turn says the next number in sequence (e.g., A says “one,” B says “two,” C says “three,” and so on). Instead of numbers that begin or end with 7 or are divisible by 7, one must say “boom.” Whoever fails to notice and says, for example, 17 (instead of saying “boom”) is out of the game. The last one remaining wins. Instead of seven, one may choose, for instance, three, and then “boom” is said for numbers beginning or ending with 3 or divisible by 3.

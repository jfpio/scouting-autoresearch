Two small groups set out into the terrain for 10 minutes and hide some objects. Then they meet at the assembly point and set off to search for the object hidden by the other group, tracking by the signs left behind (a piece of string or similar). The first group to return with the found object wins.

The number of signs, the distance between signs, and the terrain should be as uniform as possible and determined in advance.

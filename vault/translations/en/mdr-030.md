The patrol goes first, followed by the others, which on the way try to outflank it and, knowing in advance where it will hide, meet it there themselves. If they do not arrive in time, they come later but from another direction and approach the patrol, which posts only one sentry. The game ends when they spot each other. The game is only worthwhile when it spans a distance of several versts.

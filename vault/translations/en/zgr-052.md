A game widespread throughout Poland (other names: Blind Granny, Blind Man’s Bluff, Kutyubabka); suitable for children’s gardens and elementary schools. The “Granny” has her eyes blindfolded, after which she is asked a series of questions:
— Where are you from, Granny?
— From Pacanów.
— What’s the news there?
— Heavenly words.
— Is the bread big there?
— As big as your head.
— Will you eat mushrooms?
— I will.
— What do you prefer: clapping, thumping, or tiptoeing?
— (If the Granny chooses clapping, the children, running away from her, clap their hands; for thumping—they lightly tap her on the back; tiptoeing means running quietly on tiptoes). The Granny sets off in pursuit; whoever she catches becomes the next Granny.

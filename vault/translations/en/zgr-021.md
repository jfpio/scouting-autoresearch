A game from certain rural areas of Lesser Poland, suitable for girls' secondary schools. The girls form a circle, moving while singing; in the center stands the widow, and on the circumference are her daughters. They sing similarly: *d e a f*, *l a m e*, adding appropriate movements each time; then *y o u n g*. At this, the widow, who was limping during the previous verses, begins to hop. Finally, she leaps out of the circle over the arms of her daughters; the one whose right hand the widow jumps over takes her place in the center.

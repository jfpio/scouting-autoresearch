Form parallel lines with 4 participants each, spaced across any terrain at a distance of 50 meters apart. Individual participants should be positioned within up to 10 meters of each other, so they can pass a message sequentially using spoken sounds (tit-tit-taa, etc.) without excessive strain. The instructor delivers a message, in the form of words or a single sentence, to the first boys in each line. The last boys return the message to the instructor, who by then has appeared at the ends of the telegraph lines. It is permissible to note down the received message at each station. However, ensure the message is not passed on paper. Do not allow direct contact between two nearest neighbors or verbal communication. Announce this rule and unconditionally remove from the game any neighbors who do not comply. In cases of blatant violation, disqualify the entire line. The same game can be conducted using whistle signaling (each player must have a whistle). In this case, increase the distance between boys to 100 meters.

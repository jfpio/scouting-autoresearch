**Nationalities.** (Vocatus). Each participant takes a name (preferably a native one, such as: Krakowiak, Ślązak, Mazur, Litwin, Podlasiak, Hucul, etc.). Everyone crouches over a small hole in which a ball lies. The called-out player strikes the others as they flee. Whoever is hit receives a mark (if the striker misses, the thrower gets the mark). Whoever has 10 marks goes "mushroom picking."

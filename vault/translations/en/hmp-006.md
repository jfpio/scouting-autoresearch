The patrol leader places **20** or **30** small objects on a tray, table, or floor (for example, **3** different types of buttons, pencils, corks, scraps of cloth, nuts, pebbles, penknives, pieces of string, pictures—whatever is at hand) and covers them with a cloth or coat. They list these items and prepare a chart with the scouts' names to record their answers about each object. Then, they uncover the items for one minute so the scouts can see everything before covering them again. Finally, the patrol leader takes each scout aside individually and has them whisper into their ear what they remembered from the objects they saw; the patrol leader then records this under the scout's name on the chart. The scout who remembers the most items wins.

Players form a double line (pair behind pair). In front of the pairs stands an unpaired player. The last pair splits up and runs along the sides of the double line to rejoin in front of the lone player. The lone player tries to outrun one of them and take their place in a pair. If successful, the slower runner stands alone at the front.

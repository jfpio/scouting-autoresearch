After leading your patrol outside the town and showing them a certain track, offer a reward for the best and most accurate drawing of the imprint (track) of the left (right) foot. Scouts may follow the track for a short distance until they reach ground where the tracks are most clearly imprinted.

A variation of this game will be drawing horse hoof tracks, including measurements of stride length and (based on these) identifying the horse's gait—whether it walked at a walk, trotted, or galloped.

A game of rural children in the Kraków region, also known in the Ropczyce district (Lesser Poland) as *"Buhaj"* and in Greater Poland as *"Zajączek"*. Suitable for elementary schools. The children form a circle, holding hands. In the center of the circle is a player who runs in all directions, trying to break the circle by striking it with their torso where the hands are held more weakly. If they succeed, they switch places with the one who let them through on their right side.

Each of you should know how much you weigh and remember that number. Additionally, you must know the length of your span, foot, height to the knee, to the waist, your full height, and the length of your arm span (these last two measurements are almost the same). Next time, as an assignment, each of you will bring 6 length measurements—those I gave in centimetres—and now we will try estimating the weight of objects. Tell me, for example, Czesiek, how much does the chair you are sitting on weigh?

Two small groups stand in a limited space, facing each other. Each group has its own judge. The judges pin a silhouette of a leaf cut from colored paper onto the backs of the participants. At the whistle, the groups move toward each other, trying to see and recognize the sign on the backs of the opposing group without revealing their own.

Whoever identifies the leaf communicates it in a whisper to their judge, who records a point for the group. After 3 or 5 minutes, the points are tallied to determine which group has won.

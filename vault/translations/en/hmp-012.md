The Scoutmaster (acting as the king) gives one of the scouts, as the courier, a letter with the order to infiltrate besieged Zbaraż—which may be any town, village, or house—and return with proof of delivery. The courier must wear a colored ribbon, **60** cm long, pinned to the arm. The courier sets out from a distance of at least **6** km from "Zbaraż." Scouts playing the roles of besieging Tatar and Cossack forces, in order to track the courier down, may choose any positions, but no closer than **300** m from Zbaraż (it is best to mark clear, easily visible boundaries). A Cossack or Tatar encountered by the judge within these boundaries is disqualified from the game. The courier may use any ruses except disguising as a woman; however, the courier must always wear the aforementioned colored ribbon on the arm. The courier is only considered captured when the enemy removes the ribbon. The game may last up to **10** hours. Within this time, the courier must deliver the royal letter to the Zbaraż commander and return to the king with proof of delivery. Each besieger earns **3** points if they capture the courier, but loses the same number if they fail.

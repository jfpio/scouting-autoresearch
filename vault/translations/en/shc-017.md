In the afternoon period, a game such as "the deer hunt" was organized, in which one of the players, the "deer," left the camp equipped with 12 soft balls. Twenty minutes later, four "hunters" set out after him, following his tracks, each with one ball. After covering a kilometer or two, the deer would typically hide, attempting to lure the hunters into an ambush, since any hunter hit by the deer with a ball was considered "gored to death by antlers." If, however, three balls struck the deer, he was deemed killed.

Who hasn’t played this? At the finish line stands one boy—covering his eyes with his hand while he counts: one—two—three; during this time, the troop, initially lined up in a row at a distance of about 25 steps from him, approaches. When the boy says “three,” he uncovers his eyes, everyone freezes in place, and whoever is caught still moving, even the slightest bit, must return to the “start.” The first one to approach the “wizard” in this way and touch him becomes the wizard himself.

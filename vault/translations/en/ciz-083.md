The patrol leader asks one of the scouts to leave the room, then chooses a word for the remaining scouts to guess—a word representing an object, place, or the name of someone present or a famous person, etc. The scout who left then returns and, to guess the word, asks questions in turn to everyone present, to which only "yes" or "no" may be answered.

For example, the word chosen to guess is: »Mickiewicz«. The following questions help in guessing:

1. Is it the name of an object? No.

2. A surname? Yes.

3. Is it someone present? No.

4. Are they living? No.

5. Are they a leader? No.

6. A painter? No.

7. A scholar? Yes and no.

8. A poet? Yes.

9. One of ours? Yes.

10. One of the three bards? Yes.

11. A Lithuanian? Yes.

12. Mickiewicz? Yes.

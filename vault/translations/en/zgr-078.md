A game of rural boys from Podhale, suitable for elementary schools (as an introduction to the game of 'Poprz'). The playing field is as in 'Poprz'. Participants (5—20) do not divide into teams but all run back and forth across the boundary line, where the 'village mayor' catches them. A caught player switches places with the village mayor.

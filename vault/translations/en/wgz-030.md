I. Cubs stand in groups in rows. In front of each group, at a certain distance, lie potatoes and knives. At the signal, the first ones run out, peel one potato, and return to their place; the next ones run out. The group that finishes first and peels the potatoes best wins.

II. Group race. All Cubs peel potatoes simultaneously. The group that peels the most and best within the set time wins.

The above races are suitable for conducting on an outing or at a gathering "with cooking," when the peeled potatoes can be boiled.

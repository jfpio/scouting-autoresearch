The cub groups stand in rows. The first cubs receive little bags. On the signal, they place the bag on their leg and carefully, so the bag does not fall, walk forward to the designated spot and return to their groups. They remove the bag and pass it to the next child, then go to the end of the line. The group that finishes the race first and drops the bag the fewest times wins.

Complication: The cubs receive a small ball and a little bag; they place the ball on the back of their hand and the bag on their leg.

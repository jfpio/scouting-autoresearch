Kim's Game is widely known (see Kipling's *Kim*). Its variations primarily involve increasing the difficulty to different degrees. One can either increase the number of objects to be memorized, progressively shorten the observation time, or select objects that differ little from one another (e.g., various types of nails, pins, and "thumbtacks").

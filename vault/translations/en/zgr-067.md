A game described by Gołębiowski, with an application similar to 66. "Persons standing in a circle hold a tied ribbon with both hands; one person inside the circle walks around until they give someone a slap on the hand, while the others quickly withdraw their hands. Whoever fails to withdraw in time takes the place of the one who slapped."

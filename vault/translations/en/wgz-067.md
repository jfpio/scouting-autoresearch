Cubs divide into threes and form small circles, holding hands. The «Dog» chases the «Rabbit», which, while fleeing, runs into one of the circles, touching any of the cubs. That cub in turn flees, and the rabbit takes their place.

The dog must chase the new rabbit, which again defends itself by darting into another circle, and so on.

When the rabbit is caught, the roles are switched.

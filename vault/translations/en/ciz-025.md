A Scout, playing the role of an escaping Siberian, runs in any direction across snow where tracks are clearly visible until he finds a suitable hiding place in which to conceal himself. The rest of the patrol sets off 20 minutes later, following his tracks until they reach his hiding place. When they come within a snowball’s throw, the hidden Siberian aims snowballs at them, and each hit player steps out of the game. The pursuers also try to hit the Siberian with snowballs. The Siberian is allowed to take refuge in a tree, and must be hit 3 times before he is considered 'dead.'

In summer, the game changes in that the Siberian attaches tracking irons and fills his pockets with corn or peas so that on rocky or muddy ground, where tracks disappear, he can mark his trail by dropping a grain or pea at each step. Snowballs are replaced either with pine cones or tennis balls.

Due to excessive similarity, the Lion Hunt is not included as a separate game.

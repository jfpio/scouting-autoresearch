Everyone shows their right hands to the observer. Then, from under a blanket lying on the table, they extend their hands, intertwined in such a way that it is difficult to recognize to whom they belong based on their direction. The observer must identify this by the shape and color of the hands. The game can be played in teams.

Variations: identify shoes previously observed, displayed through a door crack, or tracks left in the mud after observing the shoes beforehand.

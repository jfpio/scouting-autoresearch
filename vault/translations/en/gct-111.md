In undulating and varied terrain, we establish one or more signaling stations operating in a certain direction, concealed from other sides.

Patrols dispatched from a certain point are to detect enemy stations, observe them, and report back the content of the message, specifying the station's position. The signaling station operates for a set time from 15 to 30 minutes (depending on the size of the exercise area), transmitting the message at 2—3-minute intervals. The content of the message is always the same.

The patrol that brings back the correct content of the signaled message the fastest wins.

The game leader may also serve as the signaling station.

Patrols noticed by the station fail their task—however, they should still read the transmitted message.

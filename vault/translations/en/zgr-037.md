A game described by Gołębiowski, similar to the following ones, suitable for primary schools and the lowest grades of secondary schools. Draw one fewer small circle on the perimeter of a large circle (on the playground with a furrow, indoors with chalk) than the number of players. Players stand in the small circles, with one player inside the large circle. At their call: "To the circles!", the players change places; whoever is left without a circle goes to the center.

(Gilcraft 12/20). At the beginning of the gathering, the boys are informed that orders preceded by the words: "by order of the comrade" (or others as given by the patrol leader) should not be carried out. Whoever makes a mistake and executes the order loses a point. The game continues until 3 points (or otherwise—as agreed).

In the Książnica - Atlas (Warsaw, Nowy Świat 59), "blind maps" of Poland (without inscriptions) are available for purchase. Various types of games involve drawing on the map cities, rivers, mountains, industrial centers, and the like. These maps are very inexpensive. The one who makes the fewest mistakes wins. The game can be varied: Where would I place the Scout Troop Leaders' School, and why here? How could railway lines in Poland be laid out in a wiser way than they are now?

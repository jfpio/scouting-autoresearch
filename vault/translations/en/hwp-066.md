Over a distance of 1–2 km, scouts hide on both sides of the road, no more than 50 paces away from it. Each scout uses ground depressions, rocks, bushes, etc., to better conceal themselves. After 10 minutes, the patrol leader walks along the road, and if he discovers anyone, he signals them to stand up and join him.

Each scout hides in secret from the others, but if someone notices another’s hiding place, they must not reveal it.

If the patrol leader does not find everyone after walking the road, on his return he orders one of the scouts to search for the remaining ones. This can only be the scout who swears he does not know the hiding places of the others. The winner is the one who is discovered last.

During the search, no one is allowed to leave the road.

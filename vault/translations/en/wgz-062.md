This game can be conducted on school grounds, especially if several floors are available. It will also work well in other terrain.

The troop divides into two equal parties.

The first party’s task is to carry letters to a designated place and hand them over to a specified person. They defend themselves against encountered enemies by stopping and putting on the cap, which makes the Cub invisible and forces the enemy to retreat and hide while the Cub in the cap slowly counts to 10.

In the cap, one must not move forward.

The second group hinders the delivery of the letters, trying to touch as many Cubs from the first group as possible (it is not allowed to touch those standing in the cap, but one must retreat at least 10 steps from them). Those touched retreat to the starting point and try their luck again, or leave the game, depending on the agreement. For moving while wearing the cap on one’s head, one is removed from the game.

The first group wins if they deliver more than half of the letters. In difficult terrain, victory is counted for delivering even a single letter.

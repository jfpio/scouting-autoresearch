In a disaster, several people have sustained identical injuries. A competition between teams for the best and most efficient rescue.

Such competitions—whether in the form where several teams simultaneously rescue their wounded or where they take turns attending to the same individual—should be organized an infinite number of times during first-aid training. Only practical application can benefit scouts. During the competition, no one should be allowed to say, "Now I would do this or that"; everything must be carried out practically.

Have one patrol sit so that the soles of their boots are clearly visible. The second patrol is given 3 minutes to observe. Then, the scouts of the first patrol are allowed to leave, except for one who is instructed to take several steps on soft ground, prepared specifically for this purpose. Afterward, the scouts of the 2 patrol are called back and, based on their observation of the boots, are asked to guess whose footprint it is. Naturally, the second patrol does not know which scouts were told to leave and who was asked to stay to make the footprint. A good exercise in winter. It can also be practiced with a single patrol divided into 2 parts.

Usage and application as previously described. Four or more boys may play, one of whom stands in the center while the rest position themselves on the circumference of a circle, several paces apart from each other. Those on the circumference aim at the boy in the center with a ball; whoever misses switches places with the center player. This serves as preparation for *Ekstra*.

Set up at a distance of 100—500 metres (on wheels 2—3 times farther). Each patrol leader arranges their own patrol as for a relay race with flags.

Transmitting a dispatch over a longer distance may be carried out by several teams. The team leader is then responsible for ensuring that the dispatch does not experience any delay within their sector.

A game for urban and rural children, quite common in Lesser Poland; in Mazovia known as "Tag". Application as 54. Very similar to the previous one. Played with a "nest" or without. One player chases, the others flee. When they catch someone (i.e., tap them on the back with a hand), they immediately flee themselves, and the caught player chases. Additionally, there are two variants: "round-up with retaliation," where it is allowed to immediately return the tap, and "round-up without retaliation," where this is not permitted.

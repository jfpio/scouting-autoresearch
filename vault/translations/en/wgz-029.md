Cubs stand in small groups. On the table, we prepare appropriate tasks. We distribute slips of paper with instructions to the cubs, with the first group having the same exercises, the second group likewise, and so on. At the signal, everyone reads their assigned tasks and hurries to complete them. The group that performs the tasks most neatly wins.

Examples of instructions: Sew on a button. Repair a hanger. Mark your letters, darn a sock, make a patch, and so on. — A similar exercise can be conducted as a test of skills for the Little Seamstress proficiency badge.

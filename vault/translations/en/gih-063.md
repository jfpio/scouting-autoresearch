(Gilcraft, 23/23). Someone begins the game by saying: "Marek went to the fair and bought himself an axle"—then turns to the next player, who repeats this and adds one more item besides the axle. Each subsequent player repeats the previous player's sentence and adds one more item of their own. Whoever does not make a single mistake—and remains the last—wins, earning the maximum points. The others, in reverse order of elimination, receive correspondingly fewer points.

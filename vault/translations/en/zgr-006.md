A game for rural and urban youth in Mazovia and the Lublin region, suitable for elementary schools and lower grades of girls' secondary schools. A circle of girls spins to the rhythm of a song, intoned by a participant standing in the center who seeks someone to swap places with. Depending on the responses above, either the search continues or the center player exchanges places with the one addressed, after which the game proceeds in the same manner.

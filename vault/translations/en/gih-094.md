(Gilcraft 18/21). Players sit in a circle. The patrol leader names a letter, for example, C, and asks the nearest Scout: "Name of the ship?" The Scout gives a reasonable name starting with "C" and asks the next: "Captain’s name?" Further questions, asked in turn by the next boys: "To which port is it sailing?", "What is it loaded with?", and so on. The letter may be changed over time. Whoever makes a mistake is eliminated—or loses a point.

Groups stand in rows. Circles (bowls) are drawn 10 paces in front of each group. Between the group and the bowl lie as many potatoes as there are children in each group. The first cubs receive a spoon. At the signal given by the troop leader, they place their left hand behind their back and, with their right hand, scoop one potato onto the spoon and carry it to the bowl, then pass the spoon to the next cubs.

The group that gathers its potatoes first wins.

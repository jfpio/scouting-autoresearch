Everyone except one conspires and informs him that they belong to the Marmot Club, to which he too may be admitted with certain ceremonies. All put on their overcoats, and then the novice is secretly attached with a whistle on a string to his back. Afterwards, he is told that a whistle is circulating among the gathered, on which they whistle from time to time. He must catch the whistle in someone’s hand to be admitted. Not everyone quickly realizes that the whistle, on which everyone is whistling, is carried on his own back.

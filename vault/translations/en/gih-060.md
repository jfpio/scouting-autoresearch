Each boy in turn listens to the “ticking” of 4—5 watches, which the patrol leader holds to his ear, allowing him to examine them. Then the boy turns away, and the patrol leader presents the same watches to his ear from behind, but in a different order. Finally, he must determine the order in which the watches were presented.

A game for rural children in the Kraków region, suitable for elementary schools. Around the circumference of a circle, the positions of all players are marked (spaced several paces apart) with pegs, small stones, or (indoors) with chalk. Then, they leave their places and run around the circle several times. At a given signal, each must stand by their own peg (stone, mark). Whoever makes a mistake or is late forfeits a token. In some villages, this game is called "Cranes."

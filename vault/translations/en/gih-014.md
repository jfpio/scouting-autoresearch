A long-known game. The patrol walks along a street or road and must observe the greatest number of details previously indicated by the patrol leader. Each member, upon spotting a given object, reports it to the patrol leader in a whisper. These details may be more or less distant, for example: a man with a load, an antenna across the street, a cigarette box on the sidewalk, a coal cart, and a puddle on the roadway.

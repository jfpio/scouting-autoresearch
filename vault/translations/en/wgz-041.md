This game is best played on an outing in the bushes. The Cubs form a circle, and the guard stands in the center. Then the girls scatter within a radius of 50 or more paces. At the agreed signal, they approach, hiding behind the bushes.

The guard, looking around, points with their hand at the spotted Cubs. Those pointed out sit down in the exact spot where the guard saw them. The one who approaches the guard unnoticed or to the closest distance wins.

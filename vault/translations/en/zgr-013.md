A rural children's game from the Łomża region, suitable for elementary and secondary girls' schools. The children join hands and form a semicircle (solid line in the illustration). Then, one of the end players begins a procession along a serpentine path (dotted line in the illustration), leading the others, while the opposite end remains in place. After circling the semicircle from behind, the children pass under an arch formed by the hands of the other end player and her neighbor, then return to their original formation. Next, the other end begins the same procession, and so on.

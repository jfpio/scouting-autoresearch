Popularity and application as previously described. Two boys choose a target (a tree, a stump, a ditch) and place a roughly spherical field stone near it. Then, from a distance of about 20 steps, they take turns throwing small stones, aiming to hit the spherical stone so that it rolls toward the target. Each player has two projectile stones. Whoever misses loses a projectile; when both projectiles are lost, the player loses the game.

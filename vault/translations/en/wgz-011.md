Cub Scouts scatter through the forest in search of 5 different yellowed leaves.

At the whistle, they return and arrange their leaves by colour—from the lightest to the darkest.

The group in which more Cub Scouts have arranged the leaves correctly wins.

Variation for younger ones: From the collected leaves, arrange a carpet for the ‘Queen of Autumn.’

The group that arranges the most beautiful carpet wins.

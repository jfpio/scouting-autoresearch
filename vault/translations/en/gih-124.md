The boys make a cross out of cardboard, in which the wreath, circle, and fleur-de-lis can be changed (attached) — and are kept in reserve — in silver and gold colors. The patrol leader displays this cross in various versions (different parts are attached to it), after which the patrol must write down which ranks of crosses they saw (for the Tenderfoot rank, this skill is not required in full).

Each Cub draws a slip of paper with the name of one cardinal direction. The leader draws a large circle—a compass—and calls out any cardinal direction. The Cubs must stand in the correct place before the leader counts to ten. Anyone who makes a mistake loses a point.

A more challenging version involves the Cubs positioning themselves relative to the leader’s place in the circle, with the leader then serving as “north.”

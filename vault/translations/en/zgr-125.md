The dissemination and application are as previously described. Both variant a) and b) of the previous game can also be treated as a game between two camps under the command of mothers. After "measuring" with the bat (see General Part, V a), one of the camps stands on the "winning" side, and its players serve and volley 3 times in a predetermined order (the weakest first). For the safety of those catching, the very tips of these cones should be left blunt (cut off). The change of goals (i.e., the transition of the volleyers to the "losing" side and vice versa) occurs: 1) after the kiczka is caught by a player from the opposing camp; 2) after "knocking out" a l l volleyers (by failing to volley 3 times, or by hitting the bat with the kiczka, see the previous game). Points ("kiczki") are, of course, counted not for individual players, but for the camps.

(Gilcraft, 26/24). A game similar to "bum"—but more complicated: "bum" is said to replace numbers beginning with, ending with, or divisible by 7, while "bam" replaces those for 5. For example, 17 — bum, 15 — bam, 57 — bam - bum, 75 — bum - bam, 35 — bum - bam - bam, and so on.

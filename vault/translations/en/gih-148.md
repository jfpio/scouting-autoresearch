The patrol forms a circle, with each scout assigned 2 letters—one scout has no place and stands in the center. The patrol leader signals 2 letters, and immediately the scouts designated by those letters exchange places, while the one in the center tries to take one of their spots. If successful, they receive that scout’s letters, and the "homeless" one moves to the center.

One of the girls runs in a certain direction, leaving agreed-upon signs behind her. When she finds a suitable place to hide, she leaves a note with the following content: "Find as much food suitable for birds as possible."

At a previously designated time, a group sets out following the signs. When they find and read the note, all the young scouts scatter to search for various seeds, fruits, and the like. The scout who brings the most wins.

The content of the note can be changed to vary the game, for example: "Climb a tree," "Who will be the first to reach the old oak standing alone in the glade?"

The scout who wins leaves the signs for the next game.

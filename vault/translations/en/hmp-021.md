The Scoutmaster will show a series of photographs or sketches of objects in the vicinity that every one of his Scouts should recognize, provided they have their eyes open: such as, for example, a crossroads, a window of interesting shape, a turret, a weather vane on a roof, a tree, a reflection in water (of which building?), or the like. Once the entire patrol (in pairs) is trained in this game, they can be encouraged to challenge other patrols to competitions.

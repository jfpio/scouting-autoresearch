## Translated text

We choose a letter. Then, each participant tries to compile as many names as possible of people known from literature, or generally those who have contributed to culture—or to the fight for Poland’s independence—or to scouting—or the names of athletes, and so on. Next, each person in turn reads out the names they have listed, while the others cross out those that appear on their own lists as well. The person who ends up with the most uncrossed names wins.

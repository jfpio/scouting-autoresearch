## Translated text

Exercises sharpness of sight, trains in identifying plants, and enriches memory, or rather reinforces the memorization of botanical names.

Scouts may be instructed individually or in pairs, by bicycle or on foot, to go in any direction they choose and bring back certain specified (rarer in the given area) plant species. For example, 10 tree branches, or 10 wildflowers, or 10 poisonous plants. When selecting plants, one should use a handbook describing the geographical distribution of plants in our country.

The patrol leader or judge will award points for the fastest return with the requested plant.

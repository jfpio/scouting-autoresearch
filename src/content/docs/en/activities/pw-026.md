## Translated text

On the glade, whose sketch I give you, I will arrive promptly at 3 p.m. along with your patrol leader, who will attempt to find you within one minute. Your task: by 3:01 p.m., you must lure the patrol leader toward you with your voice from a distance of less than 20 paces. You have 3 hours, a small shovel, and a hatchet — farewell.

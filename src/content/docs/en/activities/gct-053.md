## Translated text

Here, we can already assess greater distances, even those spanning kilometers (compare the youth's estimates with a staff map). It is possible to point out the inaccuracy of estimation and varying visibility in relation to the postures of the observed person (a soldier in war): lying down, crawling, kneeling, standing, running directly toward us, or parallel to us. Demonstrate the advantages of such postures and movements from the perspective of the soldier's safety, as well as from the viewpoint of the observer and the accuracy of their shot. Conduct this exercise practically in the form of the following game.

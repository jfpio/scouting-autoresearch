## Translated text

The game can take place in a forest, on built-up terrain, in a large hall, and the like. Two groups take part. Two cubs, each from a different group, hide 100 paces from the gathering spot. One cub has a siren, the other—a whistle.

Orally or by means of a note, the leader gives each group the task of finding their cub, indicating which whistle each of the girls has. Following the sound of the whistle, the group tries to locate the cub as quickly as possible.

The side that first brings the found cub to the leader wins.

## Translated text

One sixer stands opposite the others, and on the command given by the team leader, each Cub shows one letter of the alphabet—using semaphore or the deaf-and-dumb alphabet—so that the letters form a word. Two or three remaining groups read the word in silence. The group that reads it first writes the word on a piece of paper and delivers it to the game leader—wins.

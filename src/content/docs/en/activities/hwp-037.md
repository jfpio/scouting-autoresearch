## Translated text

One scout walks along a route marked on a map and makes various agreed-upon signs (with chalk, arranged sticks, laid stones, tied grass or branches, etc.). He records the order and type of signs on a piece of paper. The rest follow him, one scout after another, every few minutes. Each scout follows the signs and notes the ones he observes on his own paper. The last scout additionally erases all the signs. Who recorded the most signs? — In this exercise, the scouts do not track the signs but merely search for them, knowing the route of the march.

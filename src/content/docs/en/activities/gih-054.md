## Translated text

Little Jacob and his pursuer are "blind"—they have their eyes blindfolded. Little Jacob must answer "here" to every question from the pursuer. In this way, he is eventually caught. The patrol stands around them, protecting them from collisions.

*Variation:* The pursuer is the one of the two who finds a belt placed somewhere on the ground by touch. When he catches Little Jacob, he strikes him with the belt. Little Jacob whistles from time to time.

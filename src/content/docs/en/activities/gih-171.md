## Translated text

The patrol leader presents a schematic drawing of the abdominal cavity with all human organs, which have been "displaced" due to shock. After a brief observation, each person must write down what "disorders" they noticed in the drawn abdominal cavity and then draw the correct arrangement of the organs. Double points are awarded.

## Translated text

A rural children's game from Greater Poland, suitable for secondary school girls. The girls form a double line, holding hands in pairs. The last pair (6 in the illustration), having circled the double line from the side, passes under the * arms of the first pair, waving their arms above their heads. They then circle the double line again from the other side and pass from behind under the arms of each pair in turn, finally positioning themselves in front of the first pair. The same is repeated by each subsequent pair in order (thus 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 in the illustration).

## Translated text

Two groups sit facing each other in rows, cross-legged. The first group jumps up at the leader’s call of "it's burning," and the second does the same when they hear "it's falling."

We begin the game. If any of the girls makes a mistake—that is, stands up when she should have stayed seated or delays standing when the whole group is already up—

she is out of the game. The group that has the "unerring" Scout left wins.

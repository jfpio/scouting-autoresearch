## Translated text

A group sits in a circle. Each Cub has a box of matches.

The Sixer gives commands: "Arrange the name of a river using 15 matches," "Arrange a little house," and the like.

In evaluation: who is faster? whose is prettier? the whole group takes part.

More difficult puzzles: 1) arrange 5 little squares from 9 matches, 2) from 15 matches arrange 5 equal adjoining squares and remove 3 matches so that only 3 squares remain.

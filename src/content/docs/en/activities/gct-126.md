## Translated text

On a certain defined area, the wounded are placed in various locations, more or less concealed. In each location, there are as many wounded as there are groups participating in the exercise. Each wounded individual is assigned to a specific group and carries a card or any distinctive mark. From the dressing station, medical patrols set out with the task of finding their assigned wounded and bringing them back to the dressing station. Patrols consist of four young people. Patrols may use stretchers made by themselves. In competing groups, there may be several patrols.

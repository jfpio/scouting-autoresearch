## Translated text

The door of the room is opened, and the patrol is led out of the room, leaving one boy who sits with his back to the door. The boys sneak into the room one by one and sit against the opposite wall. When the boy sitting with his back turned hears a rustle, he points his finger, and the one pointed at moves to sit on the opposite side. After the game is finished, we determine who sneaked in and who did not.

## Translated text

Arrange, with the help of an acquaintance familiar to you but unknown to the boys, an accident along the route of your march in the form of: a) a passerby who has fainted; b) an unconscious cyclist who has crashed into a tree (a broken bicycle handlebar also requires repair); c) a drowning person; d) a drowned person pulled from the water, surrounded by a helpless group of rural youth, etc.

Note: In all such exercises, pay attention to: organization, division of labor — utilizing many boys for assistance, the calmness of the rescuers, speed, and purposefulness of action.

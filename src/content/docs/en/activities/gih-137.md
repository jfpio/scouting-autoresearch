## Translated text

"On the Trail," from the year 1932, presents the following game: the patrol leader reads a story they have composed, for example, about a Scout’s hike during which he needed to use a rope several times. As the patrol leader recounts these incidents, the patrol ties the appropriate knots. Whoever ties all the knots correctly wins.

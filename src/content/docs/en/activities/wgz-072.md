## Translated text

In the center of a circle formed by a group of children, three small pyramids are set up, each made of four small balls, arranged so that three balls lie on the floor and the fourth rests on top of the first three.

The children try to knock down the pyramids by aiming a large ball, while one of the cubs stands in the center and tries to defend them.

If one or two pyramids have been knocked over, the child standing in the center attempts to rebuild them after first rolling the ball along the ground as far away as possible.

The game ends when all the pyramids have been knocked down.

## Translated text

One scout is entrusted with a letter to be delivered to a designated location at a first distance within a specified time; the "Cossacks" attempt to prevent this by setting up well-hidden sentries. The "Courier" is captured if two enemy sentries touch him before he reaches the designated goal.

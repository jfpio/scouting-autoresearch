## Translated text

The patrol stands in a circle. One of the boys approaches each in turn and imitates a cat’s meow three times in front of them. The one thus "challenged" must keep a straight face and say: "Why do you meow, little, white kitten?" If they laugh at this, they switch places with the "kitten."

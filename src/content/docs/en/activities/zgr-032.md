## Translated text

A rural children's game from certain areas of Lesser Poland, suitable for elementary schools and children's gardens. Very similar to the previous one. The children choose one among themselves to be the beaver, another to be the Hunter, and the rest become hounds. The Hunter designates a place for the hounds to stay until the beaver hides. Then they search for the hidden beaver and catch it. The hound that first spots the beaver becomes the beaver in the next game.

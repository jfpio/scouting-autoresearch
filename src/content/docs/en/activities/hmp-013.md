## Translated text

Scouts form a Central African caravan; each carries spare clothing and provisions in a small bundle on their head. They march in a line, preceded by a "guide" at a distance of 200 meters, and follow scouting signs left by the guide. They build bridges across rivers, rafts for crossing ponds, lay fascines on marshy ground, and the like; they also leave signs for other caravans.

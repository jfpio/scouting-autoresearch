## Translated text

The Scoutmaster instructs Patrol A to sit so that the scouts of Patrol B can study their feet and footwear for 3 minutes. Then, he orders one of Patrol A’s scouts to walk across soft ground where Patrol B cannot see him. Finally, he calls the scouts of Patrol B one by one and instructs them to identify whose tracks these are.

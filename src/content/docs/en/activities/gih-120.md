## Translated text

“Vigilant Spirit” No. 10 from the year 1932 presents the following game: An excerpt from a supposed journal of two Scouts wandering through a certain region of Poland is read aloud. This could be a car or airplane trip across all of Poland or a walk through a city. The journal omits (as if “faded away”) the names of towns, rivers, and the like that the wanderers reach, but these can be deduced from the included descriptions and historical references. Additionally, in the form of conversations between the friends, there are questions concerning Poland’s territory, population, and other statistical data. Whoever, within a specified period of time (even outside the gathering), answers all the questions and identifies the names of all the places—wins.

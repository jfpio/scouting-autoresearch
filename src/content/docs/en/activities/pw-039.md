## Translated text

Tomorrow, precisely at 12 noon, you will receive from me on the hill at 435 meters northwest of the city a piece of ice. Do this—without leaving the hill—so that by 6 p.m. I receive from you the remainder of the ice. Your entire patrol will be waiting there. Be prepared!

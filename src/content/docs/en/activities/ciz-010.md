## Translated text

One patrol should sit so that the soles of their boots are clearly visible. The second patrol is given 3 minutes for observation. Then, the first patrol is allowed to leave, except for one scout who is instructed to take several steps on soft ground, prepared specifically for this purpose. Afterward, the scouts of the 2 patrol are called back and, based on observing the boots, must guess whose footprint it is. Naturally, the second patrol does not know which scouts were sent home and who remained to make the footprint. A good exercise in winter.

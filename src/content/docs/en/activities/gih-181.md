## Translated text

The patrol leader shows the boys a small, very simple sketch, which they are to copy accurately after observation.

*Variation:* The sketch becomes increasingly difficult, eventually a map; instead of copying a section of the map, they must determine from memory which routes, in which directions, and how far one travels from location A to B.

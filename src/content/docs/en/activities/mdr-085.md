## Translated text

The ball is passed from hand to hand. One player calls: *standard!* The boys instantly scatter and each stands motionless. The caller then throws the ball at any one of them. If they miss, another picks up the ball, calls: *standard!* and so on. If they hit, the ball continues in turn.

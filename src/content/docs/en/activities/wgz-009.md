## Translated text

Group game. Each Cub receives an envelope containing an identical postcard cut in the same way. The girl who assembles it the fastest wins.

When preparing the game, you can glue together two postcards with different content and then cut them. Before distributing the envelopes, we inform the Cubs what the assembled picture should depict.

When using this game at a pack gathering, each six receives one postcard to assemble together.

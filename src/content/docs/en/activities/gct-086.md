## Translated text

We can dictate a specific route by providing details of the terrain and the direction of communication lines. The youth then draws this route using appropriate topographic symbols at a predetermined scale and in accordance with the given cardinal directions. Once the youth has mastered drawing the symbols, we can apply this skill in exercises and games conducted while marching.

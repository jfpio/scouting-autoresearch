## Translated text

One unit advances and prepares an ambush in a chosen terrain along a designated section of the road. It selects for the ambush: ditches covered with bushes, groves, forest undergrowth, villages, etc., dispersing its forces over a certain area. This unit may have the following tasks: b) capturing the rear guard while allowing the vanguard and main force to pass.

The second unit, marching along the same road with security measures, attempts to detect the ambush and protects itself from surprise.

The ambush party wins if, undetected, it attacks the center of the enemy’s main force or takes the rear guard prisoner; it loses if discovered and possibly attacked from the rear first.

In the event of capturing the rear guard, the ambushing unit may outflank the enemy group advancing ahead of it and attack again—this time, the main force.

97.

The above game requires older, cunning, and field-experienced leaders. The instructor acts as referee and may initially command one of the units.

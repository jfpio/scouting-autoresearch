## Translated text

a) Person A arrives by bicycle;
 b) Person B enters the "history" area from the opposite side;
 c) both persons meet;
 d) the cyclist dismounts;
 e) they eat sweets (sweet wrappers on the sand);
 f) they walk together, one of them leads the bicycle;
 g) at a certain moment, a fight;
 h) the bicycle falls;
 i) one person drags the other (the slain) into the nearest bushes or into the water;
 j) returns for the bicycle and rides away.

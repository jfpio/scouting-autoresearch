## Translated text

In one corner of the room, a boy is seated with his eyes blindfolded, and next to him, within a radius of 1—2 metres, a series of objects is placed. At the opposite end of the room, a patrol is assembled. Boys are indicated one by one, who attempt to sneak as quietly as possible toward the guard and steal one object. If, during the approach, the guard points a finger in the direction of the approaching boy, that boy is out of the game. The boys take turns stealing objects. Points are calculated based on the skill of the approach and the successful acquisition of an object (suitable for team play).

## Translated text

The patrol is brought into a room where some disorder has been created: spilled ink, a crooked picture, a poorly closed window, and the like. The changes made are announced without specifying their nature; anyone who notices all of them sits down, writes them on a slip of paper, and hands it to the patrol leader. No one may reveal that they have noticed something, as this would disrupt the game for others.

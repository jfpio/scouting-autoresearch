## Translated text

Groups stand in rows. The first cubs receive little balls, which they place on the back of their hand. At the leader's signal, they move forward so carefully that the ball does not fall, reach the wall or a marked line, and return the same way to their group, after which they pass the ball to the next child, who, without waiting for the other groups, does the same as the previous one. The group wins where the ball falls the least number of times or not at all, and which returns to the starting point the fastest.

## Translated text

One group conceals itself along a road over a certain distance, utilizing terrain depressions and cover.

The second group, moving in patrol formation, observes the sides of the road. It calls out anyone spotted who is poorly concealed.

The roles are then switched. The group with the greater number of well-concealed participants wins.

Note that the concealment location must also ensure the possibility of observation.

## Translated text

A pack game. Cubs stand in small groups. At a signal, children from each group run out in turn and carry out commands, for example: cover the table with a napkin, pour tea, wash a glass, saucer, teaspoon, place the glass on a tray, and so on. For faster and more efficient execution, the cub earns a point for their group. The highest number of points wins.

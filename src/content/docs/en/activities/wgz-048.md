## Translated text

Cubs sit back-to-back in two rows at a distance of 10 to 15 metres. One cub from each party attempts to crawl across the opponents' line without being heard and carry over the assigned object.

Sentries catch the sneaking cubs as soon as they hear a rustle. A cub who succeeds in crawling across earns a point for their pack.

## Translated text

(Gilcraft, 5/18). The patrol leader makes a few simple movements (grabs their hips, bends their knees) and says: “Do this!” — and everyone does what they do. If they say: “Do that!” — no one moves. Whoever makes a mistake withdraws from the game; the last one remaining wins.

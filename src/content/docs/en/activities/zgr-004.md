## Translated text

A game played by rural girls in certain regions of Lesser Poland and Mazovia, also known in Greater Poland under the names "Lizard" or "Woodpecker"; suitable for secondary schools for girls. The formation is the same as in previous games. The girls circle around. At the word "bow," all make a curtsy to the one in the center, who, after the verse is finished, chooses one of her companions and exchanges places with her, after which the game begins anew.

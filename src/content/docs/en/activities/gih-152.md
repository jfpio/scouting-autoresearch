## Translated text

Each person is assigned 2 letters. In the center of the field lies a ball, with the patrol gathered around it. The patrol leader signals a letter (using flags or a whistle)—everyone except the one designated by that letter scatters, while the latter grabs the ball and throws it at the others. If they hit someone, they earn a point, and the person who was hit now calls out the next letter.

## Translated text

Groups stand at opposite ends of the hall. In the middle on the floor lie coloured triangles. The first groups step forward. The leader calls out the name of an object, for example, a leaf, raspberry, snow. The Cub who first picks up the triangle of the correct colour wins a point for their group.

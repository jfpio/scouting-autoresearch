## Translated text

A small group sits in a circle. At certain intervals, weapons of wild people lie before the cubs: a bow, tomahawk, etc. A cub, in the dark (with eyes blindfolded), searches for an object named by the patrol leader.

When the searching girl changes, the arrangement of the objects is altered.

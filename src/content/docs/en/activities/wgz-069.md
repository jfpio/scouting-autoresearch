## Translated text

We set up targets with a diameter of 10 centimetres against the wall. There should be as many targets as there are small groups.

The small groups line up in rows; the game leader directs the aiming exercises with small balls. The patrol leaders by their groups count the accurate throws, the greater number of which decides the win.

Aiming can be done in various stances, for example: 1) Lying prone, throwing over the head with the left hand or the right. 2) Cross-legged sitting, throwing with both hands. 3) Kneeling sitting, throws as in point 1 or 2.

We make the game more difficult by increasing the distance between those aiming and the targets.

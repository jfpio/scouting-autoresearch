## Translated text

The boy who is to identify others first examines with his fingers the roughness, thickness, finishing method, and the like of the edges of all the scouts' jackets. Then his eyes are blindfolded, and each scout approaches him in turn, while he identifies them by their clothing.

*Variations:* identify by hands, hair, face, and so on.

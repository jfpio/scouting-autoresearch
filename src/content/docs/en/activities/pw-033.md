## Translated text

Do you know that broken tree by the road to Borek? North of that tree is a clearing. The snow there is deep, up to the knees. In that clearing, light a fire at three o'clock and keep it burning until I arrive. I will be there at 3:15. Hidden enemies will try to extinguish it. Defend it.

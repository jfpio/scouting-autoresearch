## Translated text

The Cubs form a circle in a designated spot on the glade. The patrol leader gives a signal and closes her eyes. Everyone scatters and hides as far away as possible.

After a certain amount of time has passed, the patrol leader gives a prearranged signal to indicate she is starting the search. When she finds two girls, all three set off to search for the remaining ones. Each searches on her own or all three together—this depends on the agreement.

A girl spotted in her hiding place tries to return to the circle; if the searcher catches her on the way, she gets a mark. The game continues until everyone has been spotted. In the next game, the patrol leader hides and records marks for those caught. Whoever was caught three times, having received three marks, hides in the new game.

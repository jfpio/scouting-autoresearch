## Translated text

The instructor, having dispersed individual groups across the terrain within hearing distance of a gunshot, conceals himself at a location equidistant from all groups and fires a shot (using a pistol or a starter pistol). Upon this signal, the groups rush to the rescue in the direction of the fired shot. The group that reaches the instructor’s hiding place first wins.

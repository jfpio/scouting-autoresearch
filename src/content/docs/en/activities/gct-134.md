## Translated text

One patrol occupies a certain area: in front of a village, on a hill, etc.

Other patrols are on reconnaissance. We send them out every 5 minutes in the direction of the enemy's defensive line. Their task is to scout the enemy's force deployment and report on it.

The enemy deploys their group in any manner, without changing its position during observation by individual patrols. Their task is also to observe the patrols and report on their behavior.

The patrol that delivers the best, most precise written observations, while simultaneously being seen the least by the enemy line, wins.

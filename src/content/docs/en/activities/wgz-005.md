## Translated text

We cut a dozen or so postcards in half in various directions. Some of them are distributed to the group, while the remaining halves (a few more than those distributed) are laid out in an adjacent room or at the end of the hall. The cubs must not look at their halves before the agreed signal. Only upon the command do they search for the missing halves and line up, returning to the starting point in the order of their arrival, thus securing successive places in this race.

To make it easier to separate the cards for the next game, we mark one set of halves with a cross and the other with a circle.

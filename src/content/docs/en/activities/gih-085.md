## Translated text

(Gilcraft 17/62). Chairs arranged in a room serve as obstacles for ships navigating through fog—boys with their eyes blindfolded, starting from one end of the room toward a designated finish line, divided into 2 teams. Whoever touches an obstacle must circle it three times. The team whose ships reach the port first wins. Care must be taken to ensure that overly bright light does not provide a clue about the direction of the port.

## Translated text

Two players with blindfolded eyes move within a circle (which can be enlarged using sticks held in their hands). One flees, blowing a whistle every few seconds, while the other, listening carefully, tries to catch them. The caught player then flees from the next designated pursuer. In camp, instead of blindfolds, backpacks are placed over the heads.

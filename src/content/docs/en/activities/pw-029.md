## Translated text

To the north of here you will find an old pine. Lead the way to it. — That’s the one. Do you see those lower dead branches? — Set up your camp beneath it. You will have firewood and everything else from those old branches, but you must not touch the trunk. This rope is quite strong and is about 20 meters long.

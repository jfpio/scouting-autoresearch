## Translated text

When setting up camp, especially after prolonged strenuous games, it is possible to keep the weary engaged through the excitement of competition. It suffices to promise a certain number of marks to the entire patrol that, in the shortest time, gathers kindling, arranges it, and lights a campfire, using the fewest matches for this purpose.

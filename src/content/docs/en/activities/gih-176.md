## Translated text

The patrol leader presents a sketch of a certain terrain to be copied, on which crosses mark 2 locations that must be connected by signaling stations. However, a mountain (indicated by contour lines) rises between them, blocking the view—there is generally only one spot on its slope from which both points are visible. Find this spot and place a relay signaling station there.

*Variation:* There are multiple such spots, but only one meets the condition that it is not visible from the point where an enemy observer is located.

## Translated text

The patrol agrees on a single word, for example, *Mickiewicz*. One who does not know the word may ask, for instance, *Is it an object?* The patrol responds: *No.* *Is it a person?* *Exactly!* The patrol answers only with two words, from which the boy must guess the agreed-upon word.

## Translated text

The patrol leader stands in the center of a circle of boys, holding a string with a sandbag attached to the other end. He swings it in a low circular motion just above the ground, so the boys must jump to avoid the string touching their legs. Whoever jumps at the wrong time or too low, so that the string touches their leg, steps out of the circle.

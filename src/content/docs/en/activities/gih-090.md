## Translated text

As before—but instead of a word, a proverb is chosen. The guesser asks questions, which must be answered logically, and the answer must include that word of the proverb which was assigned to the respondent during the division of the proverb’s words among the players. By considering the answers, the guesser must deduce what the proverb is.

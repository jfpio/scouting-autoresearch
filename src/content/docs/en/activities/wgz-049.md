## Translated text

The troop detains one Cub from each pack as a prisoner at its outpost. The packs have their hideouts at an equal distance from its camp. Each six attempts to sneak up to the camp and free the prisoner. Cubs who are spotted and called out must retreat to their hideouts. The pack that first frees its comrade wins.

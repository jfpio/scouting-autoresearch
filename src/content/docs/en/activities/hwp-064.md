## Translated text

On a limited, enclosed space, one is to catch a Scout who, every minute, either whistles once or lights a match for a duration of 5 seconds. The pursuit is thus guided by both sight and hearing. If the pursuer does not catch the runner within the designated time, another takes over his role.

A signal and a meeting place for the assembly must be established.

## Translated text

We then mark the time within which the bivouac can be struck.

This may occur either at the moment the enemy is discovered or, regardless of that, at the arrival of a specified time. The bivouacking group wins if they slip away unnoticed from the encircling attackers. They are allowed to flee but must retreat the moment they see the enemy before them. The task of the attacking patrols will be to prevent anyone from the bivouac from escaping. Successively driving them back toward the bivouac and ultimately blocking their escape routes will be considered a victory.

The bivouac site is marked by lighting a campfire.

Fig. 21.

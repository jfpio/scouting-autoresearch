## Translated text

One group is instructed to leave their homes or the school building and reach a designated location by taking indirect routes (they know they will be tracked). Each participant in this group is followed by a member of the opposing group (disguises may also be used for this game), who waits near the first group’s starting point. The tracked individual tries to walk (running is not allowed) in such a way as to lose the tracker and reach the meeting place. Members of the tracking group, unaware of the location, can only find it if they do not lose sight of the person they are following. Limit the duration of the game. Members of the tracked group must arrive at a specified time—those who are late are eliminated from the game. Trackers brought by late-tracked individuals have fulfilled their task. The first group wins if the majority of its members reach the meeting place without being "accompanied," while the second group wins if the majority can locate the meeting place by successfully tracking members of the first group.

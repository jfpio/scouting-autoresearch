## Translated text

A scout chosen by lot, equipped with a good quantity of some grains or paper scraps (the latter being worse as they litter the area), goes along any path, marking the trail every several dozen steps by dropping scraps or grains. They may create false trails, i.e., deviate from the correct path, leave a mark, and return to the original direction. The rest of the patrol sets off after 10 minutes and tracks them by following the trail. The end of the exercise is marked by the place the tracked scout is to reach, which the rest should not know, or by the hour the exercise concludes.

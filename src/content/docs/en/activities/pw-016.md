## Translated text

Do you know that stream by the caves?

— When midnight comes, you will sit by it and untie the sack I give you. Inside is everything needed to prepare a feast for us when we arrive at dawn. The pot, I warn you, is leaky. The tea must be hot.

(In the sack: a leaky pot, 3 nails, pincers, a piece of sheet metal, tea, sugar, bread, cheese, eggs, etc.)

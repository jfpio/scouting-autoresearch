## Translated text

Several objects are arranged on the table: books, pens, rulers, and the like. Scouts observe for 1 minute, then step away while one of them changes the position of three—with practice, more and more objects. On command, everyone approaches, and each who notices the changes whispers them to the game leader’s ear. Instead of changes on the table, they can be made in the room or part of it, or even in clothing.

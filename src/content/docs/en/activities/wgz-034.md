## Translated text

Each Cub prepares a parcel to be sent by post, writes the address, and hands it to the leader (post office). Parcels are thrown into a corner onto a pile. When all are ready, they are sent by tossing from one Cub to another and so on around the room. The farther the parcels travel, the better. Those parcels that withstand the test are delivered; others are lost.

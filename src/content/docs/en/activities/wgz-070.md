## Translated text

Every Cub stands in any place on the field. One tries to hit the others with the ball. Whoever is touched must pick up the ball and try to hit others with it.

Each child tries to avoid being hit by jumping, bending down, or moving to the side.

The one aiming is not allowed to run with the ball. Breaking the rules results in exclusion from the game.

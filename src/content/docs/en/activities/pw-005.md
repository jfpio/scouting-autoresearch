## Translated text

A Scout from a patrol, e.g., the Wild Boars, is kidnapped, bound, and carried into the troop’s room (located on the ground floor) by another patrol, the Wolves, and locked inside.

Under the door, the Scoutmaster slips a note with the following message: *Be with me in an hour, I need you. Stay alert, for the wolves are lurking. You will find tools. Be prepared!*

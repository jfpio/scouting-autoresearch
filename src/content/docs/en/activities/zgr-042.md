## Translated text

Players standing around the circle at marked spots adopt the names of various people and objects found in an inn (innkeeper, innkeeper’s wife, bread, water, milk, meat, hay, oats, etc.). A "traveler" arrives and calls out these names in turn, at which the named players stand up and switch places. At the call "the inn is on fire," everyone switches places.

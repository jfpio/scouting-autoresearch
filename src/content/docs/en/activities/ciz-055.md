## Translated text

The game takes place at night and in the countryside, or alternatively outside the city. Two scouts are sent in a designated direction with shining torches. After **2** minutes, the entire patrol sets off in pursuit. The scouts with torches must uncover the light every minute to signal their location, but for the rest of the time, they cover their torches. The scouts with torches turn in different directions, which aids their escape, especially when the torches are uncovered at different times.

The pursuing scouts must stay together or communicate with each other using passwords and signals, for in the darkness, they might easily mistake a friend for an enemy.

For often, when one of the pursued is in danger, the other, having hidden their torch, blends in with the pursuit and facilitates their companion’s escape.

## Translated text

A picture, for example, of a camp where tents stand without trestles, tree shadows point in different directions, a flag is hung upside down; or a picture of a street in winter—yet people are walking in summer clothes, trees are green—but there is an ice rink, and so on. One must notice these "nonsenses."

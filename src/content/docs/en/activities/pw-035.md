## Translated text

Do you see that branch on this beech tree—yes, the one 5 meters above the ground? Beneath this tree, I place a full mess tin of water. Take this rope and do as I command. Bring that water up to that height immediately, without spilling a drop from the mess tin, and then bring the water back down, keeping the mess tin always with you. He who is bold will succeed!

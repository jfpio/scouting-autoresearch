## Translated text

The patrol forms a circle. The patrol leader stands in the center, points to one of the boys, and signals a letter to him. The boy must respond by signaling the next letter in the alphabet. Whoever makes a mistake loses a point. The more points someone loses, the more often they should be prompted to encourage thinking.

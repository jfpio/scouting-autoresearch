## Translated text

The patrol sends one boy out of the room, announcing that upon his return, he will have to guess what is going on. When he comes back—everyone precisely imitates his movements and words. He must then deduce that by this imitation, they are trying to distract his attention from the fact that everyone, without exception, has, for example, their jackets unbuttoned or a piece of paper pinned to their lapel, and so on—and that "this is precisely what it is all about."

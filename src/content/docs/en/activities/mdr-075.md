## Translated text

The hares do not run immediately at the call and wait until the dog chases off somewhere. Then they run to the placed stick and tap it **3** times. If the dog arrives first and taps **3** times, the hare is caught. The next hare can redeem him by calling: “for me!” and taps **3** times, then “for Staś” (etc.). The same repeats until the dog interrupts him. The last one caught becomes the dog. (Patrol game).

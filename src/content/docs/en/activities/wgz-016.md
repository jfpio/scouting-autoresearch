## Translated text

The patrol leaders step out and confer. They are to issue a command, each to their own group. The command cannot be spoken; it must be shown by gestures. The content and form of the command are the same for all groups. The patrol leaders return and demonstrate to their groups what they are to do.

The group that first correctly guesses and properly executes the command wins.

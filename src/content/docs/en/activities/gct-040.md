## Translated text

Two boys set out into snow-covered terrain, pulling sleds loaded with food, cooking utensils, and the like. After an interval of 15—30 minutes, the rest of the expedition follows, attempting to reach the campsite by tracking the sled marks left behind. The game will be more challenging during a light snowfall.

Along the way, the messengers with the sleds perform various tasks and encounter adventures, such as lighting a fire, cooking, eating (leaving behind bone remnants), hunting (traces of feathers and blood), repairing the sleds (wood scraps), or having their sleds overturn, and so on.

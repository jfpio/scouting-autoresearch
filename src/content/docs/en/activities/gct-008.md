## Translated text

We stop the class on a hill that offers a wide and varied view. The teacher asks: Where is our village visible? Where is the church tower? What church is that? Where is the town hall? Where are your own homes? Where does the road lead? Who sees sheep, cows, a woman digging potatoes, and so on.

Older youth should also determine: a) the position of a given object in relation to the cardinal directions, b) the distance of a given object from the observation point.

It is also possible to request a drawing—a sketch made using agreed-upon symbols.

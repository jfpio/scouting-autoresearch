## Translated text

— Now, draw the Arabic numeral nine (9) on the ground so that the diameter of the circle in this number is 2 small steps, and the line is 10 steps long. Good, now run around the circumference of that circle for 100 steps, then along the straight line of the nine. — Go! On the double, march!!

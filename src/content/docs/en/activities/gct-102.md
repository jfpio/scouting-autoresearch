## Translated text

Several groups participate in the game simultaneously. In each group, there are several participants wearing headgear (representing dashes) and several without (representing dots). Initially, the instructor calls out letters, later words, and finally sentences, while the youth arrange themselves in lines or rows, forming the pattern of dashes and dots corresponding to the called words. Accuracy and speed of arrangement are scored. Leaders familiar with the alphabet are appointed to organize and manage the lines.

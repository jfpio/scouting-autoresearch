## Translated text

Groups stand in rows. In front of each group sits a doll (a Cub in full uniform). At the signal, the first from each group runs out and removes the beret from the doll, then returns to the end of the row. The next ones run out and remove the bows (sashes, shoes, socks, etc.). Each girl knows in advance what she has to do.

Once the doll is undressed, the same Cubs dress it again. The group that undresses and dresses the doll the fastest wins.

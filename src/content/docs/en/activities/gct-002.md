## Translated text

Finding terrain objects, such as bridges, culverts, signposts, roadside shrines, wayside crosses, boundary stones, certain buildings, forest boundaries and clearings, road intersections, and the like. This game must take place over larger areas. A more detailed description of the given object may be provided, or only a slight hint for recognizing it among other similar ones. It is also possible to require an exact report on the condition of the given object.

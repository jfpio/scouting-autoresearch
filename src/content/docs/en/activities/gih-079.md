## Translated text

(Gilcraft 26/50). Players: 1 bull, 1 matador, 4 toreadors, 6 people with sashes. Phase I: The bull enters the arena with 5 strips of paper (8 cm in length) hanging from its back. The toreadors try to tear them off. Anyone touched once by the bull "dies." The people with "capes" try to distract the bull from the toreadors, who may run outside the arena no more than 3 times. Only one strip of paper can be torn off each time. Phase II: When all the strips have been torn off or the toreadors have been eliminated, the bull is "blinded" by tying a sash below its waist like a tail, and the matador tries to tear it off while avoiding being "touched."

## Translated text

Designate 2 trees through which each boy aims at the third, then the fourth, and so on, walking while maintaining this line. After 15 minutes, he sits down. When the entire patrol, sent individually at 5-minute intervals, arrives at approximately the same spot, the patrol leader determines who best maintained the direction. To avoid scattering too far, set a goal such as a crossroad, the edge of the forest, or a stream bank. The same exercise for older scouts is done using a compass.

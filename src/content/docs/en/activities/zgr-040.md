## Translated text

This game differs from the previous ones in that the participants stationed in their places take the names of various flowers. The gardener calls out: — Oh! They ask him: — What’s the matter? — My heart aches. — For what? — For the rose (tulip, etc.). Then the rose hurries to the gardener’s place. If someone calls out: — My heart aches for the merry gardener — everyone changes places.

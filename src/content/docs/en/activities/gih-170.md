## Translated text

Teams consist of 3 boys. One of them is injured, lying on an inaccessible rock in the mountains. He signals a message designated by the patrol leader with his hands, receives a response from the team, and then, after overcoming certain obstacles such as: jumping over a table, walking in balance along a rope stretched across the floor, and the like, reaches the injured person. After performing the appropriate rescue (all as a race), he carries him back on a stretcher. Scoring is triple or quadruple (signaling, obstacles, rescue, speed).

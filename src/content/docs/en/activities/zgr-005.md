## Translated text

A rural girls' game from the Kraków region, similar in application to the previous one. A circle of girls moves around, with "Juljanka" in the center. While singing, Juljanka performs the actions assigned to her in turn; finally, she stands up and chooses one of the participants, who then becomes Juljanka—and so it continues.

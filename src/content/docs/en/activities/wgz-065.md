## Translated text

Mark the boundary of the forest (polygon, circle) where the wolf—one of the children—resides. Cones lie in the forest. Cubs enter the forest to collect cones but, upon spotting the wolf, flee. The wolf tries to catch them but must not step outside the circle. Children keep running in and out, attempting to gather as many cones as possible. A caught child stops participating in the game. The cub who collects the most cones wins; in the next game, they become the wolf.

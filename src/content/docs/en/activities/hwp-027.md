## Translated text

We stand on a hill from which there is a fairly extensive view. The question is asked: «Where is the wayside shrine surrounded by trees?» Everyone eagerly scans the area. A shout — someone has spotted it and points in that direction. Now they describe its exact location, and for the discovery, they receive a point. We turn to the opposite side, then the question: «Who sees the black cow?» Eyes scan the distance, everyone observes intently, someone has found the cow. «Where is the statue by the road? Where is the footbridge over the stream?» and so on. Ten such questions, always one point for each discovery. Who will collect the most?

For the skilled, small, hard-to-spot objects are chosen; for beginners, easy ones.

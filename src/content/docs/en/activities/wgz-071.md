## Translated text

One of the children is the hunter. They put on a sash and receive a ball. As soon as the game begins, the hunter tries to hit one of the game with the ball. The child who is hit puts on the sash and becomes the hunter. The hunter is not allowed to run with the ball; instead, they position themselves so it is easy for them to tag the game, and they are allowed to pass the ball. The last one remaining of the game wins.

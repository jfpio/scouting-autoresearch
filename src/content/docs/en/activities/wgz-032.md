## Translated text

The troop leader gives commands: Jump, sit, tie a knot, fall in, and the like. If the words "The leader commands" precede the order, the command must be carried out. If these words are not spoken, the group stands still.

One of the cubs is a dragon that bursts from its cave and captures any cub who moves.

This game should be played briefly and quickly. After three commands, the prisoners may be freed.

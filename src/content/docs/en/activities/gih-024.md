## Translated text

(Gilcraft 8/19). The patrol forms a circle around the patrol leader, outside a line drawn on the floor with chalk. On the command: “to the water!” they jump inside the circle. On the call: “to land!” they jump out. If the command “to the water” is given when everyone is already “in the water,” no one moves—and the reverse. Two mistakes eliminate a player from the game. The last one remaining wins.

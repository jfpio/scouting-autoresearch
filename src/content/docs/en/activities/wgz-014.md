## Translated text

One of the girls leaves, and the remaining ones choose a word, for example, *excursion*. They divide into groups according to the division of the word into syllables, and upon the return of the absent one, at a given signal, they simultaneously pronounce the previously chosen syllable. The task is to recognize the syllables and guess the word.

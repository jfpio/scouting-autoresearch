## Translated text

Cub Scouts are in gym uniforms. Socks, shoes, and the Cub Scout uniform are neatly folded, each six in its own corner. Then they lie down on the floor and fall asleep. When the leader calls "fire!" each six dresses and runs out to the adjacent room. The first six that stands in a row fully dressed wins.

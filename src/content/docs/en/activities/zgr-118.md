## Translated text

A game described by Gołębiowski, still beloved by school youth in Mazovia to this day. Suitable for secondary schools. Players divide into two camps, each led by a "mother." Lot decides which camp goes to the center and which to the perimeter of the circle. Those on the perimeter pass the ball over the heads of the central players and, at an opportune moment, try to tag one of them. If they miss, a change of positions occurs. If they hit, the tagged player catches or picks up the ball and aims at the perimeter players, who scatter. If successful, there is also a change of positions; if they miss, the game continues with the original arrangement. The camp that achieves more marks (for tagging opponents) within the agreed time wins.

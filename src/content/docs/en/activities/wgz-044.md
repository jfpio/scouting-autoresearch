## Translated text

Cubs draw a large ring. One group stands on the "wall" as guards. The other group approaches the guards, and each attacker performs certain movements. The guards must imitate them but are only allowed to move along the wall. The goal is to distract the guards and sneak into the fortress. If, after a set time—for example, 5 minutes—the majority have managed to break into the fortress, the besieging group wins.

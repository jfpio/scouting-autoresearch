## Translated text

On a sheet of paper, each person writes a short word chosen by everyone—vertically down the left side and vertically up the right side. For example, with the word *dog*, the letter "d" on the left corresponds to "g" on the right, and so on. Then, each person constructs words beginning with the left-side letters and ending with the corresponding right-side letters. In our example, the 1-th word might be *cigarette*, and so forth. When everyone has written their words, each takes turns defining the first word so that everyone else can guess it. The first to guess correctly earns a point. After everyone has defined the first word in turn, they proceed to define the second, and so on. The person with the most points wins.

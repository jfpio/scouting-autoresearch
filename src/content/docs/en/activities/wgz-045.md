## Translated text

A narrow passage, such as a door, or two trees at a green assembly point, serve as the entrance to the port. Two girls with blindfolds act as cruisers and stand on either side of the entrance. Other cubs, as enemy ships, try in turn to slip through so quietly that the guarding ships do not stop them.

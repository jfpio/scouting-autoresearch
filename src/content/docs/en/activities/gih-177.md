## Translated text

On a sheet divided into 12 to 16 rectangles, various conventional symbols are written in these squares. It is shown to the boys for 1 to 1 and a half minutes.

During observation, the boys must copy the sheet, placing descriptions in the appropriate squares instead of the conventional symbols—explaining what they represent.

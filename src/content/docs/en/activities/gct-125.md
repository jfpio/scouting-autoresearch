## Translated text

a) using a "chair" made from the hands of two people, b) by means of a rescue grip, c) using improvised stretchers made from previously prepared material.

The group that first transports its sick (running is not allowed during transport) a certain distance wins. Penalty points are given for: shouting, dropping the sick person (damaging the chair or stretchers), and running.

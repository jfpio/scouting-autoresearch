## Translated text

Groups of 4 to 6 scouts—"sinking." The patrol leader calls out, for example, "square knot," and begins counting consecutively, for instance, up to 10. If the ship captains manage to tie a square knot from the ropes they have within the counting time, they save their sailors. If not, one of them sinks. Then the patrol leader calls out: "fisherman's knot," and the same process repeats.

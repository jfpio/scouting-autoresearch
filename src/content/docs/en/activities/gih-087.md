## Translated text

(Gilcraft 27/24). Each player is allowed to voice their guess once. The patrol leader describes an object using ten descriptors, each subsequent one narrower than the previous (e.g., if describing a duck—the first descriptor: "bird," the last: "says quack-quack"). Whoever guesses correctly writes down on their sheet at which descriptor they guessed and scores accordingly. For example, if guessed at the fourth descriptor, they award themselves 6 points. Naturally, at the end, everyone states their guess for verification. The game is played, for instance, to 60 points.

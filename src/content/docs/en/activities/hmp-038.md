## Translated text

Scouts go out individually, in pairs, or in a patrol. In the city, they seek out the elderly, women, and children in need of assistance. Upon returning, they report modestly and without embellishment on what they managed to accomplish. In the countryside, they offer themselves to peasants for work without pay. These same exercises can be carried out as races ("a good deed race").

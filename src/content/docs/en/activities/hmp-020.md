## Translated text

The Scoutmaster sends scouts one by one into a room for half a minute; each, upon exiting, must list the furniture and other objects they noticed. The winner is the one who observed the most. The simplest way to record the items will be to prepare in advance a list of objects on a table divided into columns, labeled with the scouts' names (as in Kim's Game).

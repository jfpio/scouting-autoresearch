## Translated text

A rural girls' game from the Kraków region, suitable for primary schools and the lowest grades of girls' secondary schools. A circle of girls spins, with the "gardener girl" in the center. The gardener girl then selects one from the circle, and the game continues until all have been chosen from the circle.

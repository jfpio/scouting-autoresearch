## Translated text

The patrol sits in a circle. The patrol leader asks individual boys a series of questions. The one questioned is not allowed to answer—this is done for him by his neighbor on the left, meaning each boy answers the questions asked of his neighbor on the right. Whoever slips up loses a point—or gives a forfeit.

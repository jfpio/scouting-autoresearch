## Translated text

You will cleverly find out about the "Eagles" patrol of the neighboring troop: when the Eagles hold their meetings,
where,
what happened at their last meeting,
what they plan to do at the next meeting,
how many are in the patrol,
what the troop leader’s opinion of them is,
whether they have an agreed-upon writing system (alphabet, cipher),
what their alarm plan looks like,
what inventory they have,
and whether they have, and what they have, in their corner of the troop’s room.
I give you 3 days to do all this.

## Translated text

This is a game, like the following ones, played for forfeits. The instructor places a ring or a small hoop on a sturdy string and ties its ends with a simple knot. The players either stand or sit in a circle, holding the string with their hands, passing the ring from one to another, or, by continuously moving their hands, pretend to pass the ring. A Scout standing in the center searches for the ring, and when they find it with someone, that person replaces them in the center and gives a forfeit. If the ring is caught between two players in the middle, the one who did not want to accept the ring goes to the center of the circle.

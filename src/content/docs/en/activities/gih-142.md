## Translated text

The “Expedition” sets out—“for real”—onto the playing field, or “pretend” with a pencil on paper, and receives “via radio” the order to head in various directions of the world. Whoever correctly plots the directions or marches on the field earns positive points. The same applies in the field and in the forest.

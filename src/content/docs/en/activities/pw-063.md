## Translated text

In that hollow below, a wolf patrol is encamped. — Approach it with silent step, find out what is happening there, write everything down, and return to me. Be careful, for the jingles (bells) I have tied to your legs may easily betray you, and if they do, the wolves will give you a thorough thrashing.

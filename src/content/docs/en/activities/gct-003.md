## Translated text

The leader hides one or more persons within a certain area, preferably in a forested underbrush. A story about the missing persons in the wilderness should be created. Rescue expeditions will be carried out by troops or individuals.

Note regarding games: 1, 2, and 3.

This can be an individual or group search. The group that gathers first at a given object or person (called together by the group members with agreed-upon shouts) wins. The group that arrives at the location in full strength is considered the first.

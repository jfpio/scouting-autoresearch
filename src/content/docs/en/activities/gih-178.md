## Translated text

Each boy has on cardboard, divided into 12 rectangles, a drawn sketch of fairly simple terrain, such that each rectangle contains a different conventional symbol or several. Someone "holding the bank" has in a bag a corresponding number of squares for each sketch—a number of small rectangles identical in drawing to the squares on the sketches. Taking such a rectangle in hand, they show it to everyone, after which the one who has such a square on their sketch must state what the visible conventional symbol means, then receives that rectangle. The first to cover their entire sketch with these rectangles wins (a standard game of "lottery").

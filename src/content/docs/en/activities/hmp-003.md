## Translated text

One of the scouts slips away as a fugitive from a labor camp and flees through the snow in any direction toward some hiding place. There, he conceals himself from the guards, who have just discovered his disappearance. The patrol allows him at least **20** minutes to escape before beginning the pursuit, following his tracks. As they approach the hiding place, the fugitive starts pelting them with snowballs. Anyone hit by the fugitive steps aside as if killed. The fugitive must be hit three times to be considered captured.

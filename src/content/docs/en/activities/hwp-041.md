## Translated text

Two scouts go into the field with an electric torch. Two minutes later, the rest set off in pursuit of them. The pursued flash the light every minute, constantly changing position in order to escape the chase. If things go badly, they separate to divide the pursuers; the one without the torch tries to draw as many pursuers as possible to himself in order to help his comrade. Both fugitives should previously agree on signals between themselves. The torch passes from hand to hand, always to the one in less danger.

Within the designated time, the one carrying the torch must be surrounded and taken prisoner. The area designated for the exercise should be quite spacious but bounded.

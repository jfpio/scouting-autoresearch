## Translated text

Beware: there is no water around here. Take the shards of an old pot from this sack. Repair the pot and store the water from my canteen in it until tomorrow; you will find the tools in the sack. Act quickly, for we must continue our journey soon.

(In the sack: a clay pot broken into 2–3 pieces, wire, pliers, a hammer.)

## Translated text

This is necessary for: setting up a kitchen, pitching a bivouac or camp; in water journeys, and often also for orientation.

We determine the wind direction by tossing dry grass or paper upward. Their descent, deviating from the vertical, will indicate the wind direction for us. To ensure accuracy, the obtained data should be verified several times.

When setting up a camp for a longer period in open spaces, the prevailing wind directions in Poland should be taken into account, i.e., NW, W, and SW.

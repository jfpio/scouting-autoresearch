## Translated text

From among the group, 3 to 5 young scouts are chosen in such a way that the others do not know about it, and they are tasked with finding the secret order. Whoever already has the order in hand passes it to the next initiate, and thus the order must pass through the hands of all the initiates in turn. The last one hides it.

The entire group tries to uncover the initiates and take the order being passed.

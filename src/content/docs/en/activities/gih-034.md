## Translated text

In the park, each boy chooses a person to sit beside and observes them discreetly. The most sensibly constructed account of who the observed person might be, their profession, where they came from, why they are sitting there, and so on, wins a reward. Attention must be paid to the boys' proper conduct toward those being observed.

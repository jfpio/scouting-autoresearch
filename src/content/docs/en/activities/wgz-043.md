## Translated text

One group forms a circle. The Cubs stand at a certain distance from each other and cover their eyes.

In the center of the circle stands the enchanted princess.

The other group tries to enter the circle, reach the princess, and touch her to «disenchant» her. Whoever manages to do this without a sound wins.

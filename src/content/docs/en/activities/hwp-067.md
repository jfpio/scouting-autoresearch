## Translated text

This exercise shares the same characteristic as the previous one: one person hides while the others search, or all the White Patrol hide while the Red Patrol search. One party must wear distinctive badges. It is essential to clearly mark the boundaries of the area where hiding is permitted (on a map, by pointing out the limits from an elevated vantage point, or by partially walking the perimeter), as well as the duration of the exercise. The area may cover several square kilometers, and the exercise may last several hours. It should also be determined how many found within a given time constitute a win. A "prisoner" is taken by touch (or by calling out from a distance of 15–20 paces).

A rule may be in effect that once a hiding place is chosen, it cannot be changed, or that in case of danger, one may leave it for another. After the exercise, those who remain undiscovered must report who they saw near their hiding place.

## Translated text

A cap is hung on a stick. At a distance of two metres, two boys run at the whistle to snatch it. Run slowly from the left side only and grab with the right hand (so that they do not collide and injure themselves). The one who is late may, in the first moment, snatch the cap from the opponent's hands. The whistle should be blown from the centre. A long whistle means: "Attention!" and immediately after, a short one: "Go!"

## Translated text

The instructor plays the role of the game, while the scouts move away from him by 100 steps, drop to the ground (preferably on undulating terrain or sparsely overgrown with bushes), and by crawling, attempt to get as close to him as possible without being seen by the instructor. If he spots any scout, he orders that scout to stand up and cease playing. After some time, he gives a signal with a bugle or a password, everyone stands up, and the one who crawled closest unnoticed wins and receives marks.

## Translated text

(Gilcraft 18/48). The patrol stands in a circle, with an apple lying in the center. One of the boys leaves the room—he will be the thief; another is designated as the gardener in the thief’s absence. The thief returns to the room and must seize the apple, escape from the circle by the same path he entered, and avoid being touched by the gardener—without knowing who the gardener is.

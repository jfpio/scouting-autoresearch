## Translated text

The group is divided into two lines, which are positioned—lying down—opposite each other at a distance of 8 to 20 steps. One line (the youth lie at intervals of 2 to 8 steps from each other) orally passes a random sentence from one end to the other, ensuring it is accurately repeated at the opposite end of the line. The second line, lying opposite at the same intervals, attempts to hear and reconstruct this conversation. Then, the tasks are reversed.

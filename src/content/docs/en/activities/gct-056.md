## Translated text

Place one of the groups, preferably a patrol, in varied terrain (undulating with cover). Conceal them using available terrain features at various distances (from 200—1200 meters) from the observers (minor movements of the concealed in their positions may be allowed). Call other groups in turn to the observation point. Participants observe the opponent within a defined sector of the terrain and assess the distance for effective shooting (sector boundaries are marked using terrain landmarks). Observation time is limited. The group that locates the greatest number of concealed individuals and best assesses the distance to them wins.

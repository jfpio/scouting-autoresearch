## Translated text

A march guide is chosen, who receives from the leader a secret instruction on where to lead the entire unit according to the map. (The route should be varied and conducted along secondary roads.) The remaining participants receive maps (aim to assign a map to each unit). They do not know the destination of the entire unit. Their task is to indicate at any moment, upon the leader’s request, the unit’s position on the map and to report changes that have occurred in the terrain (point out discrepancies between the terrain and the map’s situational and vertical depiction). The leader scores and evaluates the orientation results based on the answers received from individual march participants. The march guide should be rotated.

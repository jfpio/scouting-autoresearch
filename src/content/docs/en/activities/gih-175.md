## Translated text

The patrol leader arranges stacks of books on a table to form what resembles mountains—larger books at the bottom, with progressively smaller ones toward the top. The boys then create a contour-line sketch of this "terrain"—the upper edges of books of medium thickness represent the shape of a contour line, while the next book on top represents the next contour line. Thus, these "valleys" and "mountains" are marked as on a map—using contour lines.

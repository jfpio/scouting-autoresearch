## Translated text

A group takes part in this game.

The patrol leader says: «40 paces to the south lies a stone. Look for the letter hidden beneath it.»

Under the stone is a note: «Search beneath the hazel bush growing beside the thickest tree you can see from here.» Beneath the hazel bush is an order to seek a hollow tree to the east. The next note points to a path leading to the hiding place where the treasure is concealed. The young scouts begin the search in pairs; they should use a compass.

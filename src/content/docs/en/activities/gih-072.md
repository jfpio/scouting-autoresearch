## Translated text

(Gilcraft, 38/68). Two teams stand on either side of a line drawn with chalk, each boy with his right foot on the line. On the signal, the mutual tugging of competitors begins. When both feet are beyond the line, their "owner" has been pulled over and must assist his victor.

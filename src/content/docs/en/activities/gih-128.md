## Translated text

The patrol leader reads out the addresses (surnames and positions only) of 4 letters in which a scatterbrained secretary has mixed up the positions and surnames of the following individuals: the troop leader, the Scout Region Commander, the Chief Scout, and the Chairman of the Z.H.P. The boys must sort this out: properly address these letters. For example, the letters read: Asst. Patrol Leader Michał Grażyński; Asst. Chief Scout — Janek Czupiralski. They must correctly match these surnames and positions.

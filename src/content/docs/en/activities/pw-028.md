## Translated text

Do you have a cat at home? No? — all the better, for I need a cat just for a moment. Bring me a cat from wherever you can, without harming it in any way.

You’ve brought one! Well done, scout. Now pay attention, for I am letting it go free, and you must quickly sketch the path it now takes as it heads home.

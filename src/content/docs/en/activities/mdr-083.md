## Translated text

Players hit the ball against the wall. Whoever catches it gets a throw (right hand — 2 throws, left hand — 3). If they miss, they go to hit the ball themselves. If the ball slips from someone’s hands while catching, that player is "sparzony" (burned) and must go hit the ball.

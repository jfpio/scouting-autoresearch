## Translated text

A selected scout sits on a stool with eyes blindfolded. The area chosen for this game must be covered with dry twigs, leaves, gravel, and pebbles. A second scout, approaching cautiously from a distance of 90 meters, must traverse this space in 1½ minutes and touch the seated scout before being heard. Naturally, before the game, the selected scout is required to give their word of honor to adhere to the truth.

## Translated text

Cub groups stand in rows. A few paces in front of each group, a rope is secured; further on stands a long bench, and still further, in a marked circle, lies a scarf. At the leader’s signal, the first cubs run, crawl under the rope, run further, jump over the bench, then toss the scarf over themselves and return to their place; the next ones do the same. The group that finishes first wins. On hikes, natural obstacles can be used, such as: a fence, a ditch, a stream.

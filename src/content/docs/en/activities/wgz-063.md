## Translated text

The entire group, except for one six-person patrol, forms the four walls of a room, leaving space for a door. This house stands in the American forests. The Indians are to raid tonight. Three white settlers enter the room, lie down to sleep, removing their belts, footwear, and the like. They carefully fold their clothes and blindfold themselves, for it is night.

The remaining white settlers from the designated group stand outside on both sides of the door.

One of them whistles. The "sleepers" dress with their eyes still blindfolded, find their way to the door, and escape before the walls slowly count to forty.

The white settlers waiting outside grab each other’s hands, remove their blindfolds, and flee together.

The walls turn into Indians, chase the fleeing settlers, who are safe once they cross the marked boundary (e.g., a grove).

Each group takes turns playing the role of the "white settlers."

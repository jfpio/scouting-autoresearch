## Translated text

After leading your patrol outside the town and showing them a certain track, offer a reward for the best and most accurate drawing of the imprint (track) of the left and right foot. Scouts are allowed to follow the track for a short distance until they reach ground where the tracks are most clearly imprinted. A variation of this game will be drawing hoof prints of a horse, including measurements of the stride length, and based on these, identifying the horse's gait—whether it walked, trotted, or galloped.

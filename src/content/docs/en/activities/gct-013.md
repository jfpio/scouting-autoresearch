## Translated text

A. seated in.), B. moving.),

On small, open terrain, all listeners blindfold themselves. On larger terrain or at night, the game proceeds without blindfolds. The winner is the one who first discovers or catches the whistler.

C. 3 groups, each with whistles of different pitches, hide or flee from three groups, each of which pursues only one unit assigned a specific pitch. The fleeing groups must have a predetermined escape time and a location where they will hide after the final whistle. The pursuers should locate the correct group. After being found, the individual groups switch roles. Each whistler should initially be assigned a different direction of escape to avoid confusion and to not hinder the pursuit.

## Translated text

Each patrol taking part in this expedition, having equipped themselves with sledges, loads them with food, cooking utensils, sheepskin coats, shovels, and the like, and in turns, all scouts pull them forward in pairs. Ahead, **2** scouts proceed for some **2** kilometers before the supply train and mark the route, sometimes with the tracks of their boots, sometimes with various signs.

The rest pull behind them, read the signs, and upon arriving at the site, build huts from snow using shovels, with sticks serving as supports and brushwood to reinforce the walls and roof.

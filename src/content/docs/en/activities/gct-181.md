a) fastest transport of the patrol over a distance of 1 km (2 bicycles per patrol),
b) determining directional angles of roads at the Crossroads (using a Bźard compass — 2 compasses per patrol) and transferring them onto paper in the form of a sketch (two executions per patrol),
c) setting up sentries around the bivouac,
d) gas alarm (everyone has masks),

f) rifle shooting (military shooting range — 3 shots at a distance of 100 m),
g) crawling over a distance of 25 m,
h) while lying down (firing niche), bandaging one’s own hand (small bandage for each participant).

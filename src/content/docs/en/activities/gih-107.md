## Translated text

(Gilcraft 9/61). Four teams in the four corners of the hall. Two of them—opposite each other diagonally—attempt to convey a question and a sensible answer to one another, but not through gestures or signaling. At the same time, they strive to raise an enormous uproar to hinder the communication of the other two opposing teams. The first pair to successfully communicate wins.

## Translated text

In the woods lying outside the city, a common and uncivilized phenomenon is the littering of papers. Pointing out that this should not be done will be good and beneficial for the future.

We recommend collecting papers abandoned in the forest. Children gather them in groups at one location. For hygienic reasons, they should not be allowed to collect them by hand. For this purpose, we instruct each child to cut a stick and sharpen its end—picking up papers is only permitted with the stick. The group that collects the most papers within a set time wins.

An extension of this activity could be burying the collected papers or burning them.

## Translated text

The patrol leader should prepare a collection of small objects, such as various penknives, fabric samples, pencils, pens, stones (rocks or minerals), booklets, and so on.

For beginners (in the first few games), only 15 objects are arranged, while for those more practiced, 20—30. After placing the objects not too close to one another on a table, they are covered with a cloth.

Then, the scouts are instructed to sit around the table so that each can see well, and the cloth is lifted for 1 minutes, after which the objects are covered again. Instead of watching a clock, one may count aloud at a moderate pace to 60.

Afterward, each scout must prepare a list of the remembered objects or recite them in a whisper to the patrol leader, who notes them down with marks on a previously prepared name list.

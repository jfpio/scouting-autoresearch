## Translated text

Two escorts lead the wrongdoer before the judge, who sits on a chair placed on a table. A blanket hangs down from the table, behind which one boy is hidden; the wrongdoer stands on a rug—the blanket, one edge of which is under the table, behind the hanging blanket. The court scene takes place, and when the judge loudly proclaims the verdict: “perish!”, the boy hidden under the table pulls the “rug”, causing the “condemned” to fall flat; the escorts then catch the falling person, who must be very careful—and carry him away.

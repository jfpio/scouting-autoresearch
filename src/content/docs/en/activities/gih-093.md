## Translated text

The patrol sits in a circle. Someone tosses a handkerchief to another Scout and calls out, for example, A. That Scout must immediately answer 3 questions: What is your name? Where are you from? What is your profession?—and the answers must start with A, for example, Adam from Afghanistan, artist. Whoever fails to answer loses a point.

## Translated text

A variation that facilitates sneaking while making capture more difficult is the ability of "Peowiaks" to disguise themselves. This type of game, full of excitement and requiring ingenuity, is easier to conduct within youth organizations, though a teacher can also organize it during afternoon games. This game type can also be applied at a colony or camp once warmer relations with the local population have been established, from whom youth can easily borrow the necessary clothing. The goal of sneaking could be: a town square or camp—a clearing in the forest. Ensure that the youth return the clothing to its owners immediately.

For example, here are some of the more interesting forms of disguise observed, both in the city and the countryside: beggar, lady, village woman, herdsman with cattle, coachman or passenger, hiding in a cart with straw, laborer with a handcart, disguising oneself as an instructor-judge, chimney sweep, gossiping women, arrival by boat of people dressed in rural attire (camp by a lake), Jew, blind man with a dog, and so on.

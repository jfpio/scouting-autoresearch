## Translated text

A person has been kidnapped for ransom. The pursuit sent out finds a note on the road with the words: "To village N — — signature."

The pursuit will consist of patrols, setting out in succession from a certain point. They locate, according to the map 1: 100.000, designated rest or overnight stops, about which the captured person informs by leaving messages in the form of letters.

In village N, further information must be obtained with the help of a discovered sign, letter, or by conducting an interview with people who indicate the bandits' further route.

Subsequent messages may be as follows: a) a small forest east of point 103, b) a mill on the river M, c) lake P, the mouth of stream K, etc.

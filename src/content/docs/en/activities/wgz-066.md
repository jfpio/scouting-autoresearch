## Translated text

One of the girls is the "blind grandma." She has her eyes covered and holds a small bag containing various types of fabrics. A girl caught by the "blind grandma" must ransom herself by selecting, through touch, the named type from the bag. If she fails to recognize it within the set time or guesses incorrectly, she becomes the blind grandma.

Other types of ransom may be used, such as: the caught girl draws the requested object from the bag, for example, a hazelnut, a rubber band, and the like.

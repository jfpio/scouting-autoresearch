## Translated text

In any area where the leader has identified the presence of natural tracks—such as those of a hare, wild boar, roe deer, adder, crow, or other creatures—participants proceed to search for them. The instructor roughly defines the area or trail lines for the trainees. Teams report the location or path of the tracks either orally or in writing, precisely specifying their position. It is also possible to request a sketch of the trail’s course or drawings of the tracks.

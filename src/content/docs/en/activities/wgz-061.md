## Translated text

The game takes place in the field. Cubs divide into two parties: the reds and the yellows. Each party has an equal, specified amount of ammunition, e.g., 12 cones each, arranged in a row at a distance of 1 step from 1 another.

Half of the cubs from each party surround the arsenal and defend it. The other half sets out to capture the enemy’s ammunition.

Whoever manages to seize ammunition (one cone) can no longer be pursued.

If an attacker is touched by a defender, they are wounded and must go to the enemy camp as a prisoner, but they can be rescued by a cub from their own party who leads them by the hand back to their own camp.

When rescuing a wounded cub, ammunition must not be taken.

After a predetermined time has elapsed, the amount of captured loot is calculated.

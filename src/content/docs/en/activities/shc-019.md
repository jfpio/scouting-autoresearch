## Translated text

The patrol divides into two halves. One half, with eyes blindfolded, is arranged in a line with spacing slightly greater than both arms outstretched. The other half of the scouts must sneak through this living wall from a certain distance. Both groups are allowed to make any movements, but those standing as the "wall" may not move from their spot (they may sit, kneel, or wave their arms), while those approaching may not retreat.

## Translated text

We are bandits. From our leader, we have received the order to retrieve a treasure hidden by his predecessors, about which he has the following information: a) Follow the ravine (gorge) located north of village A. Soon, you will see a huge rock. b) Stand on it so that you have the peak M in front of you, and then your right hand, stretched out to the side, will indicate the further direction. c) Walk in that direction for 20 meters to a hornbeam growing on the slope (all other trees around are beeches, so knowledge of trees determines further orientation). d) Stand on the slope above the hornbeam so that your gaze, directed over the first branch of the hornbeam and simultaneously to the right of the main trunk, falls on a cottage facing you sideways. On the extension (look through the chimney of that cottage), you will see a group of 5 spruces. e) Beneath the largest one, you will find the treasure.

This may be, as in the given case, information provided in full, or individual pieces of information may be placed in lists hidden at specific locations. These lists must be found by the searching groups (the leader’s envoys).

## Translated text

Players take turns. Specifically, after briefly considering their choice, each must recite from memory a poem while simultaneously performing simple arithmetic operations on numbers written by themselves or provided on a slip of paper by someone else. The speed of performing the calculations and harmonizing the recitation with the counting determine the number of points each player earns.

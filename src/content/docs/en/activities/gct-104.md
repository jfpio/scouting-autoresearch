## Translated text

The patrols form rows (other formations are also permissible) with their backs to the signaling instructor, who stands at a distance of at least 150 meters from the rows. He holds signal flags and a whistle. At the first whistle blast, the last boys at the end of each row turn to face him.

The instructor transmits a letter or word to them using the flags. At the second whistle signal, they turn back to their previous position, and the next boys from the end of the row turn to face the instructor, who then transmits another, subsequent letter or word to them. This continues until all members of the row have received individual letters or words. Once the dispatch has been transmitted in this manner, the rows scatter in different directions upon an agreed signal. There, they assemble the individual letters (words) and deliver the completed dispatch in writing to the instructor. The evaluation will depend on: the accuracy of the decoded dispatch and the speed of its delivery.

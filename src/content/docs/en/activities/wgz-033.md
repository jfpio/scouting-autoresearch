## Translated text

Cub Scouts stand scattered. The leader gives a command, for example, *line up*. The Cub Scouts quickly form up; whoever arrives last loses one point for their pack. Then the Cub Scouts scatter again and wait for the next command. The pack that loses the fewest points wins.

## Translated text

(Gilcraft 4/56). Two or three teams in a circle, facing inward, spaced one meter apart, arranged so that every third person belongs to the same team. On the call “hop,” the first from each team runs to the right, weaving like a shuttle between those standing alternately to the left and right; upon reaching their place, they slap the hand of person number 2 of their team, and numbers 2 start. The team wins in which the last one slapped returns to their place first.

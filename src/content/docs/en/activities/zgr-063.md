## Translated text

A game from the rural girls of the Stanisławów region (Little Poland); suitable for children's gardens and elementary schools. The children form a circle, holding hands. After singing the song, the children scatter, and the cobbler chases them. Whoever is caught becomes the cobbler in the next game.

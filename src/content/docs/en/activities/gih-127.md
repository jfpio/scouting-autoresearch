## Translated text

The first part of the game involves the boys tracing a map of the district of the city where they live. After checking and correcting the maps, and calculating the points, the second part of the game follows: marking with crosses where the members of the patrol live. Once this part is completed—the finale: "what should the patrol's alarm plan look like on this district map" or: "what does an already prepared alarm plan look like on this map?" It is hardly necessary to add that it would be nonsense to play the above game without conducting a few real alarms.

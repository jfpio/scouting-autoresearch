## Translated text

(Gilcraft 31/25). About 12 boys sit on the ground in a circle, touching shoulders, legs stretched toward the center where one remains. Holding himself stiffly, he falls onto the outstretched hands of one of the seated boys; that boy pushes him so he tilts onto someone else, and so on. Whoever lets the Tumbler fall, fails to hold him, takes his place in the center.

## Translated text

Everyone is seated, with one person left without a place. That person walks around and tells a story, during which, whenever they say "storm at sea," everyone must jump up and switch places, while the storyteller also tries to find a seat. Anyone who fails to react to the words "storm at sea" loses a point. The person left without a place becomes the next "storyteller."

## Translated text

Boys from one team, with their eyes blindfolded, form a cordon across the room, standing with legs apart so that their neighbors' feet touch. They must not bend their knees, but they may move their arms. The others try to slip through the cordon—preferably under the legs of the cordon—without being touched by a hand.

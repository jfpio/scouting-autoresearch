## Translated text

A peasant wedding dance from the Kutno district (Mazovia), suitable for girls' secondary schools. Dancers form pairs in two rows facing each other, holding hands. The rows move together and then retreat. When they meet, they bow. Finally, as they sing: "And four dynas came together," each dancer makes several turns, first taking hands (tour de mains) with her vis-à-vis, then with her partner. Afterward, they retreat back into their rows. The step is a mazurka step.

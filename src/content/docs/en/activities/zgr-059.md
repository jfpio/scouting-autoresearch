## Translated text

A game for rural children in Łańcut County (Lesser Poland); application as above. The player chosen as the "dunker" sits in a ditch (or in a designated area on the field marked as the "pond") and pretends to be asleep. The children approach and provoke him. Whoever he catches becomes the dunker. (Older children may also play this game in the water during bathing, under appropriate supervision).

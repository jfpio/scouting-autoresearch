## Translated text

On a card divided into 12—16 rectangles, letters in Morse code or semaphore, as well as in the regular alphabet, are drawn in some of the squares. The patrol observes the card for 1—1⅓ of a minute, after which it must reproduce it from memory, replacing signaling letters with regular ones and vice versa. For each correctly written letter in the proper rectangle—2 points; for incorrect placement—1 point.

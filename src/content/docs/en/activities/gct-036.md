## Translated text

An insurgent from the year 63, exiled to Siberia, escapes from their place of confinement. A dispatched pursuit follows the insurgent's tracks, striving to capture them as swiftly as possible. The pursuit sets out 10—15 minutes after the insurgent's escape. There may be several insurgents. If the insurgents are captured within the designated time (e.g., one hour), they lose. In moments of danger, they may conceal themselves by firing snowballs at their pursuers. A struck member of the pursuit group is considered killed. To eliminate an insurgent, they must be hit three times.

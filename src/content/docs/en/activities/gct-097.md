## Translated text

The instructor leaves notes at various points in the terrain (road intersections, forest edges, solitary trees, hills, ditch crossings, fences near houses, bridges, river bends, etc.).

These locations are provided to the patrols, which find the designated positions using a map and locate the left notes, copying their contents. The original notes left by the instructor must not be taken. The notes may contain instructive information: about the terrain, nature, the life of villagers, their customs, etc. They may also include—as a further challenge—orders related to reconnaissance. They may be encrypted or written in Morse code.

The patrol that finds all or the most pieces of intelligence, and whose members know the contents of the left notes, wins. Verify their knowledge through questions.

## Translated text

At dawn, three scouts called "marmots" are sent out into the mountains to hide. After breakfast, the scout troop, acting as "highlanders," sets off to detect the marmots by a certain hour, for example, 4 in the afternoon. The highlanders win if they spot the marmots, even with the aid of binoculars, by accurately identifying the person and their hiding place (the "burrow"). Certain boundaries are set, beyond which both the marmot and the highlander are considered out of the game.

## Translated text

A feather—down—is tossed above a line drawn with chalk across the room. Two teams attempt to fan it with their caps onto the opponent's territory so that it lands on the ground there. Usually, boys cannot resist blowing on it, but the rule should be to fan it with caps. Boys enjoy this game for its cheerfulness and amusing tricks.

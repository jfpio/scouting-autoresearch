## Translated text

a) observation of a fallen rider (joint report on his appearance), b) reading a dispatch sent by the judge from a distance of 1 kilometers, containing instructions for the further march through terrain covered (the patrol must march in such a way as not to be seen by the judge), c) lowering the entire patrol down a cliff using a rope (application of the rescue knot). The rope must be taken on the further journey, so a prison knot must be used to allow the rope to be pulled down after the last member of the patrol has descended, d) clearing broken glass from the path (an obstacle hidden in the bushes), e) a gas-contaminated area, rescuing those affected by gas and transporting them over a distance of 250 meters, f) gathering mushrooms (a forest with a large number of mushrooms) and frying them, g) organizing safety during bathing, h) conducting reconnaissance of a ford across a river, i) defense against a surprise attack (use jujitsu).

## Translated text

The patrol forms a semicircle in front of the patrol leader. The leader says: “nose,” “floor,” “lamp,” and points with a finger at these objects, but when saying “nose,” for example, points at the lamp, and so on. The boys must, while continuously watching the leader, indicate the correct objects that are being named. Whoever makes a mistake loses a point or is eliminated from the game.

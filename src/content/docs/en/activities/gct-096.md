## Translated text

Each patrol is a team tasked with reaching a certain point. The patrol marches according to a map, having specific intermediate points along its route.

Patrols depart every few minutes. The patrol that accomplishes the task the fastest (running is not allowed) will perform best.

Initially, the task should be made easier by selecting a point that is simple to reach, such as a village or a road junction. The march will always take place along main roads.

For experienced groups, a more intricate route (even cross-country) should be used, with details that are harder to determine from the map, such as forest clearings, hills not marked with elevation points, small bridges, and the like.

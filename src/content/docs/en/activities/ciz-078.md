## Translated text

In the middle of a group seated in a circle stands the instructor, posing questions to any individual.

However, none of those questioned should answer under penalty of forfeiting a token, for to their left sits an advocate who speaks on their behalf. For example, the instructor asks A: *"Do you enjoy reading?"* B, acting as the advocate, replies: *"Indeed, a book is my bedtime cordial."* To a question directed at B: *"Are you not yawning?"* C answers: *"Oh no, for in such fine company I am not bored in the least."* If the question to C is: *"But you are B’s defender,"* and B answers *"yes,"* then B must forfeit a token. The game’s variety depends on wit, that inexhaustible wellspring of ideas.

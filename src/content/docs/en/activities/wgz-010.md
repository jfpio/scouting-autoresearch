## Translated text

A small group stands in a line. Each girl receives a ribbon several centimeters long. The ribbons should be of different colors, and they may even be in different shades.

At the opposite end of the room, the patrol leader lays out ribbons of the same colors on a table; she may also add more colors.

Whoever matches the correct color (on a given signal) lines up in the order of their return to the assembly point.

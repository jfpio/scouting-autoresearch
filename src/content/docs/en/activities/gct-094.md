## Translated text

A. Moving based on directional angles and the number of given steps, or precise meter measurements using a tape (game in a small area). The goal of this game may be searching for: a) a water hydrant in the city, b) a bird’s nest or an animal’s burrow in the park, etc.

B. Moving through city streets based on directional angles and a designated sequence of subsequent street corners, serving as new positions needed to determine the next directional angle.

C. Instead of a sketch for the entire route, we may designate only the directional angle to the next point. There, one must find a note containing the next directional angle. Then, reach the next point again, and so on.

## Translated text

When a troop establishes a camp anywhere, it dispatches scouts with notebooks to gather an outline of the surrounding area within a radius of 2 kilometers. It is possible to send 4 patrols in 4 directions, ideally guided by the compass rose. The patrol leader sketches, while the scouts measure distances by pacing and time, and bring back details such as: road bends, terrain undulations, distances, railways, rivers, ravines, gullies, embankments, dikes, ponds, landmark trees, houses, statues, boundary posts, and mounds.

The patrol that produces the best outline or chart in the shortest time wins, earning a certain number of marks.

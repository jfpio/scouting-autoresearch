## Translated text

The Guard stands in a line facing away from the smugglers. The smugglers crawl from a considerable distance, and when they are near the border, their leader gives the command: **Take cover!** A moment later, he shouts: **Fire!** The smugglers clap their hands. The Guard turns around and chases the smugglers. Those caught are out of the game. The game can be played 3 times, and the number of those caught is counted.

The roles are then swapped in the groups, and after the third game, the number of captured smugglers is compared.

## Translated text

1) Two troops position their members every 50 steps apart. By passing a flag from one to another, the runners strive to ensure their troop delivers it to the finish line first. 2) 4 troops stand at two finish lines. The first faces the second, the third faces the fourth. From the center, the troop leader releases two small flags. One runs to one finish line, the other to the other. (From the first to the 4-th team). At the finish line, they seize them and carry them to the opposite side (to the 2-th and to the 3-th team). In this manner, once all have run, the last runners bring the flag back to the troop leader.

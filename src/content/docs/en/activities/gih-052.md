## Translated text

The troop is divided into two parts. One group has their eyes blindfolded and the boys are positioned 1—2 paces apart from each other (arm's span). The other group must sneak through the wall of outstretched arms. The boys forming the "wall" may wave their arms. Anyone touched is out of the game. After finishing, the roles are switched—the ones who were sneaking now form the wall, and so on. We calculate which side has more successful sneakers.

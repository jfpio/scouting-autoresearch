## Translated text

A game for a group or two. The Brownies stand in a circle. They hold the edges of an apron or dress with their fingers. The patrol leader, standing in the circle, turns to one of the girls and calls: "hold." She should release, and only hold again when the patrol leader says "release." Whoever makes a mistake leaves the game. The "unerring" Brownie wins.

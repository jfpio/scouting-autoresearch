## Translated text

During a march: on a highway, road, or street, we instruct the youth to estimate distances in paces from their current position to various objects: trees, telegraph poles, road intersections, houses, forest edges, and so on. When determining distances along the road itself, a helpful reference will be the roughly constant distance between telegraph poles or planted trees. Estimating distances to the side of the road will be more challenging.

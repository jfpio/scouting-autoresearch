## Translated text

Two groups line up in rows facing each other at two opposite ends of the hall. The game leader scatters an equal number of white and red matchsticks across the entire hall. At the whistle, one group collects the white matchsticks, the other the red ones. The group that finishes first and lines up in its place wins.
